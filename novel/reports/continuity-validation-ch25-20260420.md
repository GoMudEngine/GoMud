### 3. Lamp Tic Arc
**PASS — correctly calibrated.**

Opening watch sequence: the hand reaches, fingers "come up off his knee," close
on nothing, open "before they closed." Aldric explicitly notes he did NOT catch
himself — the hand returned on its own without supervision. This is tic-arriving-
at-extinction, not tic-absent. That reading is confirmed by the chapter's own
framing: "He had not marked the motion" — it is becoming automatic not-to-do.

Closing sequence: lamp blow-out by breath, "pads of thumb and forefinger resting
on air." The hand did not pinch; it simply wasn't used. This is tic-effectively-
gone at the level of impulse, but Aldric still *notices* the hand, still sits
"with the not-reaching" — the arc is conscious suppression giving way to
absorption, not full disappearance. Bible says "begins to change Ch. 25,
effectively absent Ch. 29." The chapter does not overclaim total extinction;
Aldric's meditation on the lamp and the hand is still volitional, not the
unconscious automatic omission of Ch. 29. **Calibration is correct.**

One continuity note: Ch. 21 shows Aldric pinching "at nothing, at air" as a
lamp tic misfire on the road from the temple district; Ch. 24 shows him pinching
"something at his coat that was not a lamp wick." Ch. 25's opening "reached once,
in the first quarter hour, for a wick that was not there" is consistent with this
descending arc — the reach is shorter each time. No contradiction.

### 6. Sixty-Mile Debris-Field Estimate
**PLAUSIBLE — minor flag.**

Aldric's arithmetic: "Confluence to the highlands. That is — sixty miles. A
little more." The bible describes the hill as "five days east of New Plymouth"
(Ch. 22), with New Plymouth ~1 day east of Confluence by river barge (Ch. 6,
Davan's barge journey). The highland formation (Reth's site) is described as
east of New Plymouth across the highland road — the group is now approaching it
on Day 3 of 5. If the full journey Confluence→hill is ~6 days travel, and the
hill is ~5 days from NP, NP is roughly 1 day from Confluence. River barge
distance on Gaius is not specified in miles, but 60 miles for the full span
(Confluence to eastern highlands) is plausible for a barge-and-road geography
at these travel speeds.

**Flag:** The Greenford detour complicates the estimate. Brennan is in Greenford,
which Aldric reaches in four days riding east from Confluence (Ch. 17). He then
rides west to NP (Ch. 17 closing, "four days of solitary travel" per Ch. 21
opening). Greenford therefore appears to be ~4 days east of Confluence, which
would make Greenford *further east than the hill* — contradicting the bible's
geography (Greenford is a university town east of Confluence, the hill is east
of NP). This is a pre-existing geography tension in the source material, not
introduced by Ch. 25. The 60-mile estimate does not worsen it. Flag for author
review of Greenford's relative position vs. highland formation.
