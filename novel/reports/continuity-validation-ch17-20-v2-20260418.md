### Renner's "thirty years of archival work inside the temple" (Ch. 20)

**Math-check: CONSISTENT — with important clarification.**
The passage reads: "Thirty years of archival work inside the temple. Four years
of nights and cooperage meetings after."

Bible Section 2 (Renner): "~50, former temple archivist. Argues astronomical
interpretation of margin notation for 4 years."

Math: If Renner is ~50 now and began archival work at ~20 (reasonable for a
scholar entering institutional work post-training), thirty years of archival
work puts her leaving the temple at ~50 — i.e., approximately when the story
takes place, or recently before it. This is consistent with "former temple
archivist." The four years of cooperage argument also matches the bible exactly.

Cross-check against the "four years" cooperage figure: the passage says "thirty
years inside the temple, four years after" — total career to present is
approximately 34 years of scholarly work. At ~50, this places her entering
archival work at ~16, which is slightly early but within plausible range for an
institutional scholar starting as an apprentice. No hard contradiction.

The phrase does NOT claim Renner spent thirty years doing astronomical research
— it claims thirty years of archival work inside the temple (a role that would
have given her access to the Restricted Collection materials she later argues
from). This is consistent with the character as written: someone with deep
institutional access who then broke away to argue the forbidden interpretation.
The "thirty years inside" actually strengthens her as the "True Believer
struggling to accept the Ship theory" note in FB-2, because it means she spent
thirty years maintaining the institution that managed the forgetting before
defecting to the cooperage group.

Verdict: **Consistent.** No arithmetic conflict with the bible.
