## Check-by-Check Results

### 1. Day 5 Placement
**PASS.** Ch. 27 opens Day 5 explicitly ("Day five opened in a light
that did not match the day"). Ch. 26 ends with the group camped at
the shepherd's fold after Day 3 (Vane's POV timeline: follower
confirmed Day 3 east, camp that night). Day 4 is handled off-page
between chapters. The five-days-east canon (stated in Ch. 22 closing,
confirmed in bible Section 3 Geography) is satisfied.

### 2. Reth's Formation Match
**PASS.** Ch. 27 renders: 400m lateral extent ("Four hundred meters
lateral extent," Aldric's voiced account), no grain/seam ("it had no
grain. It had no fracture pattern"), manufactured surface. The curve
at the southeastern end goes "into the ground" — matching Reth's
exact phrase quoted back by Aldric in Ch. 27 ("The thing goes into
the ground, Keeper"). The disc-sized recess is at the southeastern
end. All details match Ch. 17 v2's Reth testimony precisely.

### 3. Inner Orbit Symbol "Is the Hill"
**PASS.** The bible locks: "Not carved, part of the original surface."
Ch. 27 renders: roots running *along* the lines not across them; soil
settled into the depressions; the symbol as feature of the hull at
manufacture. The sentence "The symbol was not on the hill. The symbol
*was* the hill" is the distillation of the locked canon. The recess
inner face also: "Not carved into the inner face. Part of it." Both
instances fully consistent.

### 4. Father's Remains Placement
**PASS with note.** Bible author-lock: father dead, remains traumatic
but not graphically described. Placement candidate: Ch. 27 approach,
before or during disc-recess sequence. Ch. 27 renders: pack-strap at
five paces from discovery, coat at seven paces, bones sparse (animals,
a decade), detail on coat matching Ulla's Ch. 11 description (grey
with dark facings at cuffs, grandmother's shoulder seam). Satchel at
the hand-line. No weeping on-page ("She did not weep"). Hand-line
pointing at the recess. Four paces short of the recess — the chapter
never gives a reason for those four paces, which is good restraint;
the reader supplies the weight. The "traumatic without graphic"
directive is honored.

**Note — coat color consistency:** Ulla's description (Ch. 11) gives
"soft grey, dark facings at cuffs, seam at shoulder." Ch. 27 renders
the coat as "once grey, now the color weather made" and specifically
confirms "the facings at the cuffs were black-weathered almost beyond
reading. The seam at the shoulder was a seam." This is exact call-back
to Ulla's description. **Consistent and payoff-complete.**

### 5. The Bound Book in the Satchel
**PASS with new canon note.** Bible does not prohibit this item; it

**Recommend:** Add to Section 5 of the bible under "Ulla's Letter /
Father's Letter" or as a new entry "Father's Satchel Contents."

### 6. Disc Behavior at the Press
**PASS.** The hum has been escalating across Act 3 (pulses → warmth
→ continuous hum by Ch. 27 morning → "fine continuous pressure" at
the recess). At seating: the hum *stops*. Complete cessation. The
chapter frames this as the climax of the escalation arc: "For the
first time since she had taken it out of her coat three years ago
and held it for the first time with a hand that knew what it was,
the disc was *quiet* in her hand."

Bible canon on disc escalation (Section 7, Resolved — Disc Behavior):
"amplify the existing pattern (more frequent pulses, audible hum where
currently silent, warmth intensifying)." Ch. 27 delivers all three
across the morning sequence. The stop-at-authentication is a logical
extension of the escalation arc — the disc has completed its function.
**Consistent with bible intent.**

### 7. Something-Happens Rendering vs. What's Sealed
**PASS — seal holds.** Ch. 27 reveals on-page: hum stops; sub-audible
bone-sound through the hillside; air goes interior-cool; disc symbol
lights warm color (new color, not previously produced); arcs on
hillside faintly glow through vegetation. Ch. 27 withholds: what the
ship's systems do in response; whether the orbital ships receive; what
happens inside the ship; the message container; what Davan sees or
experiences from inside in Ch. 28. The chapter ends at "The hill
answered under her. / She held on." — which is the activation moment,
not the response. Ch. 28's threshold (Davan's new color *inside the
ship*, the never-before interior experience) is protected.

### 8. CRITICAL: The Narrator-Leak Sentence
**AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED.**

The sentence: *"Somewhere above the country — above the ridge, above
the eastern weather line, above the air she was breathing in this
breath — three moons that had held position for ten thousand years
received the signal they had been holding station to receive."*

Immediately firewalled by: *"She did not know this."*

**The case for cutting (Ch. 22 red-line precedent):** The Ch. 22
"ten thousand years" narrator leak was cut on the grounds that the
POV contract is Maren's perception and the narrator does not know
what Maren does not know. This sentence does precisely the same
thing: the narrator steps outside Maren's perception to state Option
C cosmology as fact ("received the signal," "had been holding station
to receive"). The "She did not know this" firewall is structurally
identical to the Ch. 22 firewall — which is what made that passage
cuttable, not saveable. A firewall that says "she didn't know" does
not actually resolve the POV breach; it acknowledges it.

**The case for keeping (structural climax argument):** Ch. 27 is
the Act III climax toward which the entire arc has been building.
The Ch. 22 leak was ambient, mid-chapter, unnecessary for structure.
This sentence is the payoff of the "three moons that had held
position" thread that runs through the entire manuscript (the
children's rhyme, the margin notation, Reth seeing "three" in the
late afternoon sky, the face's orbital display). An argument can be
made that at the literal moment of activation, a single narrator
omniscience beat — immediately firewalled — reads as earned payoff
rather than accidental leak. The reader needs to understand that
the thing worked, even if Maren cannot yet know it worked.

**Recommendation:** **FLAG for author, default to modify rather than
keep or cut outright.** A middle path exists: rephrase the sentence
to stay within Maren's possible perception or intuition, without the
narrator asserting orbital-ship reception as fact. For example, the
sentence could be recast as something like *"The bone-sound in the
hillside was going somewhere. She did not know where."* — which
allows the reader to infer but does not state. This preserves the
climax weight without the POV breach and does not require the "She
did not know this" firewall (which is itself an acknowledgment of
the breach). **Author should decide: keep as written, cut entirely,
or modify along these lines.**

## New Canon to Fold

The following details introduced in Ch. 27 have no prior bible entry
and should be added to the bible if the author accepts the draft:

1. **Surface texture at room scale:** The hull material is "smooth
   the way the disc was smooth, at the scale of a room" — not
   polished, smooth as a manufactured default. This extends the disc's
   described surface quality to the ship-scale object.

2. **Arcs under the grass:** The inner orbit symbol arcs are visible
   under the hillside vegetation as depressions in the hull surface
   that pre-date any soil accumulation. Plant roots follow the
   lines rather than crossing them.

3. **Interior-cool air on activation:** The air between Maren's
   body and the hillside shifts on activation from morning-cool to
   "a dry held cool that had no weather in it. The air an interior
   had." This is the first described physical consequence of the
   ship's systems waking.

4. **Bone-sound register:** The ship-scale response is sub-audible
   — "a sound at the bone," not at the ear. "Deeper than the disc's
   hum had been, wider." This is the activation sound signature.

5. **Disc's warm new color at activation:** The disc symbol lights
   with a color not previously produced — "Warmer. The color of
   morning sun through a leaf." Not blue (the disc's prior light
   color from Chs. 9 and 11). This is a new color associated with
   authentication success, distinct from the escalation-phase blue
   ring.

6. **Arcs glow through growth on activation:** The hillside symbol
   arcs become faintly visible through the vegetation after seating —
   "a suggestion that the material was no longer inert." Not bright;
   a suggestion.

7. **Father's satchel contents:** Small bound book (failed oiled
   cloth wrapping), fire-tin with flint, river-stone (egg-sized,
   river-smoothed). Maren does not retrieve any of these in Ch. 27.
   The book is a potential hook for Ch. 29/30.

  she was six." This is new characterization of her relationship to

  her father.

  both in the charged register (once aloud to herself at the disc-hum
  realization, once to Davan at the recess approach). Bible confirms
  this word-pair as her voice signature for settled depth, not
  agreement or fine-ness. Both instances are correctly deployed.

- **Edvar's hug moment** — brief and wordless except for "Go" / "I'll
  come back" / "You'll come back when you come back." This is Edvar's
  functional final on-page moment before the hill sequence. No prior
  canon contradicted; worth flagging in case the author wants Edvar
  to have a Ch. 28-29 role.
