### 1. Day-3 Parallel Coverage Consistency (Ch. 25 / Ch. 26)
**STRUCTURAL FLAG — requires author confirmation, not a contradiction.**

Ch. 25 camps "at the eastern edge of Irongate country" after the face
scene at dusk. Ch. 26 describes the same travel day (Irongate transit,
mid-morning spring stop, follower detection, late-afternoon heath opening)
and camps "at the shepherd's fold an hour past dusk" — identified in the
text as the fold Edvar had picked two nights ago on "the eastern heath."
This fold is described in Ch. 25 as the camp location from Aldric's POV:
"a dry-stone fold older than yesterday's, three walls and most of a
fourth... They were at the eastern edge of Irongate country now."

The two chapters are describing the SAME fold, the SAME camp, Day 3.
**This is not a contradiction — it is the designed dual-POV structure
landing correctly.** Ch. 25 shows Aldric's experience of the camp;
Ch. 26 shows Vane's experience of the same camp, with the added Vane
action of choosing to bypass Edvar's originally-planned fold (a prior
Irongate-edge fold that Edvar had proposed) and continue to the
shepherd's fold as a tactical adjustment after detecting the follower.

The only potential confusion: Ch. 26 (lines 327-337) describes the
original planned fold as "at the east edge of Irongate" and the
shepherd's fold as "an older one on the eastern heath... an hour further
east." Ch. 25's camp is described as "at the eastern edge of Irongate
country" which MATCHES the shepherd's fold geography — heath opening
east, road running out of the gap. The "prior fold" in Ch. 26 appears
to be a different, closer fold that the group bypasses due to Vane's
tactical adjustment; the shepherd's fold on the eastern heath is where
both chapters agree the group actually camps.

**Author confirmation needed:** Is the bypassed fold in Ch. 26 (the one
they pass at line 473 — "She came back. 'Pass it,' she said") distinct
from the Ch. 25 fold, or is Ch. 25 showing an earlier camp (pre-follower-
detection) that Ch. 26 then supersedes? The cleaner reading is that both
chapters resolve to the same shepherd's fold on the eastern heath, with
Ch. 26 adding the tactical context for why they bypassed a nearer option.
No arithmetic violation in either interpretation.

**Geography check:** Ch. 26 Vane describes the bypassed fold at "the
road-verge with three walls and most of a fourth" (lines 470-476) — then
the group camps at the shepherd's fold "an hour past dusk" a mile further
east. Ch. 25 describes the camp fold identically as "three walls and most
of a fourth" and explicitly places it at the eastern edge of Irongate
country. **Risk of contradiction:** if the bypassed fold in Ch. 26 is
the same fold described in Ch. 25, then the groups camps at an
*additional* unnamed shepherd's fold that Ch. 25 does not mention. This
would mean Ch. 25's camp description is the bypassed fold and the actual
night camp is off-page in Ch. 25. This is the one live contradiction
risk. **Resolution recommendation:** the author should align which fold
is the actual night camp across both chapters, or clarify that Vane's
"pass it" bypasses a fold that Aldric's POV never enters.

## Structural Note for Author

The one live question is the fold geometry across the two chapters. Recommend
confirming one of these two readings before finalizing Ch. 26:

**Reading A (recommended):** The bypassed fold in Ch. 26 (which Vane
orders past at "Pass it") is a fold *closer to the Irongate gap exit* that
Edvar had originally proposed as a camp; the shepherd's fold on the eastern
heath (an hour further east) is where both chapters actually camp. Ch. 25's
camp at "the eastern edge of Irongate country" is the shepherd's fold, not
the bypassed fold. Both chapters agree on the camp. The bypassed fold is
a Ch. 26-only element, never entered in Ch. 25.

**Reading B:** Ch. 25's fold is the bypassed fold, and the actual camp in
Reading B is the shepherd's fold further east, which Ch. 25 doesn't reach.
This would mean Ch. 25's Aldric POV ends before the actual camp — possible
if Aldric registers only the lamp-blow moment, not the full arrival at the
further fold. This reading requires that Ch. 25's description of Vane
"standing at the mouth of the fold for a long time looking back" be at the
bypassed fold, and the chapter's lamp scene occurs before the actual night
camp is reached. This is a harder editorial ask.

**Reading A is strongly preferred** because it preserves Ch. 25's clear
"camp here tonight" structure without additional revision, and because the
description of the two folds in Ch. 26 and Ch. 25 matches the shepherd's
fold (not the bypassed one) in all details.
