- **Line ~2413 (dock scene, after "She settled back.").**
  Added one paragraph before the existing "He became aware, slowly, of the
  weight of the pine carving":
  *"The feeling sharpened, and then — before he understood it was
  happening — his skin-light came up. Not bright. A low blue-calm glow
  along the insides of his wrists, faint enough that the dock's last

  decided, not performed, the body answering a question the mind had

  pressed his palms flat against his thighs without thinking, which

  sits near him on the dock — body recognizing her before the conscious
  mind has. This pays the emotional register forward by making a
  body-event the hinge of the scene, and it connects forward to Ch. 19's
  face-activation + green-gold-at-Edvar and Ch. 28's new-color register.
  Existing characters only; no new plot.
