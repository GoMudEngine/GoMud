## Drift / soft inconsistencies

**1. Aldric's four-container count vs. five-object statement (minor clarity risk)**
In Ch. 24 Aldric says: "And in the room were five objects. Four containers. Smooth.
Dark." Then "And the fifth object was a face." This matches the bible exactly
(Section 5: "five objects: the face + four smooth containers"). No drift — but the
passage is the first time any POV gives the group the full object count (previously
Aldric described this only partially at the cooperage in Ch. 21). Worth flagging
as a check: Ch. 21's cooperage account (confirmed by re-read) had Aldric say "five
objects. One of them is a flat dark surface... It shows an image... Four of them...
One of them is not like the others." Ch. 21's account grouped them differently
(described the face first, then the orbital bodies). Ch. 24's account gives them
in order (containers first, face last). This is not a contradiction — Aldric is
giving a fuller account on the road — but a revision agent should confirm no
listener would think the count changed between the two tellings.

**2. "Patient urns" — Aldric uses this phrase in Ch. 21 interior too**
In Ch. 21 (interior monologue, line ~200), Aldric's thoughts list the chamber
contents as "the four smooth containers... lined against the wall like patient
urns." In Ch. 24 he speaks this phrase aloud to the group: "Like patient urns."
The phrase appears twice: once interior (Ch. 21), once spoken (Ch. 24). This is
not a contradiction but could read as slightly deflated — if the phrase is already
in Aldric's established interior register, its spoken arrival in Ch. 24 carries
less weight than it would if heard for the first time. Soft note for revision.

**3. Edvar's left shoulder stiffness**
Ch. 24 notes Edvar "one shoulder into the off-side leather because his left was
stiff from the bench the day before." Ch. 23 does not specifically mention Edvar
being uncomfortable on the bench. Not a contradiction (the stiffness from
sustained cart-driving is plausible without being stated in Ch. 23), but a small
continuity gap a reviser could close with a single phrase in Ch. 23 if desired.

**4. Vane "no one on the back" report — wording**
Ch. 23 (Maren POV): "Nothing on our back yet... Long sightline across that last
pasture. I looked. Nothing." Ch. 24 (Davan POV): Vane's camp circuit — "her not
saying anything meant the same thing as the morning: no one on the back, long
sightline, clear." This is consistent. However, Ch. 24 gives Vane a second
perimeter sweep at day-two camp: "She came in from the second circuit and did not
say anything." This is also described as "clear" in effect. No contradiction with
Horst's agent following. The agent is behind them, not yet resolved to visible
range — this is consistent with Ch. 26 being the chapter where Vane's nose
registers the follower. Clean.

## Two-face handling verdict

**PASS — properly handled.**

Ch. 24 manages the two-face reveal exactly as the bible requires for this
chapter: both faces are disclosed and placed side by side without the discrepancy
between them being articulated.

- **Aldric's face**: He describes it as showing "four of them. Around a world —
  blue, green, with cloud. A star at the center burning. The three near bodies
  each with their mass. And the fourth —" and stops. He does NOT describe what
  his fourth showed. He says only "Mine showed four. And a burning point. And the
  world. The near three — these three. And the fourth." He stops at "the fourth"
  both times and turns to Davan with the listening pause of someone who has heard
  Davan's account and is noting the difference without naming it.

- **Edvar's face (Davan's account)**: "Smaller. Further. Distant." These are the
  exact words from the bible (Section 5: "fourth shape appears 'smaller, as if
  further away'"). Davan delivers this description twice — once in the opening
  dialogue, once explicitly in the lamp-scene: "— smaller. Further. Distant.
  That is what I saw."

- **The discrepancy**: Aldric "did not say what his face had shown him of the
  fourth. Davan did not ask. The asking was not for tonight." The chapter
  explicitly holds the comparison open. The two accounts are "on the blanket
  together" but the comparison is deferred: "the how of it could keep until
  there was light to see it by." This is precisely the bible's instruction
  (Section 5: "Must remain distinct and consistent through Act 3 when comparison
  occurs (Ch. 25)").

- **Davan's interior**: He registers that the fourth "was the thing his body had
  gone to both times he had seen it — the far-away quality." He does not note
  that this might differ from Aldric's face. He is in his own register of the
  data, not comparing. This is correct POV discipline.

**Ch. 25 has its work intact.** The discrepancy is present for the reader
(Aldric clearly has different data) without being surfaced in-world. Strong
execution of the two-face constraint.
