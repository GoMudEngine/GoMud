## Remaining items (V3 plan)

Smaller and tighter than V2's plan. Most are 1-3 lines.

### V3-1 — Bible alignment (do first, low-effort)

The V2 Round 1 fix updated the manuscript but missed propagating to STORY_BIBLE.md. Three references:

1. **Father timeline.** Bible Section 7 line 1221: *"He went east ten years ago and did not return."* → change to "five years ago." Bible line 999: *"ten years ago, the disc will open it"* → review context (may not be the same reference). Bible line 1232: *"(animals, weather, ten years)"* → likely needs "five years." Bible line 1225: *"the disc was with his infant"* — verify whether this is independent canon or needs change. **Action:** read bible Section 7 in context, update all "ten years" references that refer to the father's departure to "five years." Verify the "infant" reference is something different (the disc-lock setting may be from when Maren was an infant — a separate timeline point).

### V3-2 — Two genuine new continuity items

2. **D4 vs Vane Ch. 8 olfactory contradiction (Major).** The new D4 canon (added in V2: passive cellular Chrysalis is universal on Gaius even in true Hollows after seventeen years of environmental exposure) contradicts Vane's Ch. 8 reading of "zero Chrysalis baseline" in Maren. **Fix:** add ONE clarifying sentence in Ch. 8 distinguishing what Vane's nose detects — the *active hosted/expressed* Chrysalis warmth, not passive cellular trace. Suggested: after "the girl smelled like a person and nothing else," add: *"What Vane read was hosted Chrysalis — the warmth of the active organism doing its work in a body. The trace cellular presence everyone on Gaius carried was below the threshold of any nose."* Or similar in Vane's professional register.

3. **Bark-skin nose-bridge in Ch. 30 exceeds D2 arc (Major).** Bible D2: bark-skin spreads from wrists upward, mid-bicep by Ch. 4, upper arm/shoulder by Ch. 18. There is no canonical arrival at the face. Ch. 30 mentions "bark grain at the nose-bridge had dried." **Fix:** either (a) remove the nose-bridge reference (let Vane breathe through her mouth for a different reason — e.g., the mountain air, the cold, or just don't explain), or (b) backward-plant ONE Ch. 26 or Ch. 28 observation that the bark has reached her jaw or temple. Recommended: (a) — cleaner, doesn't require a setup beat in already-tight chapters.

### V3-3 — Three Major+ voice items still landing soft

4. **Aldric Ch. 29 three-bullet manifesto (Medium per Vee V3 P3).** The V2 Round 1 fix converted the bullets to italicized paragraphs, but Vee V3 says the underlying three-point thematic-declaration structure persists. *"Three separate this-is-what-I-now-know statements in a row, each with an italicized hook sentence."* **Fix:** cut the third paragraph entirely, OR have the second paragraph fail to complete (italicized hook arrives, Aldric loses the thread, returns differently). Option B preserves more content; Option A is cleaner.

5. **Maren Ch. 30 moon-passage proleptic narrator intrusion (Medium).** *"the moment was also a moment she would carry for the rest of her life and would not, afterward, describe to anyone, not even to Davan"* — narrator speaking from outside the book's time. **Fix:** cut from "the moment was also a moment she would carry" through "not even to Davan." The remaining sentence still has the present-moment register Maren needs.

6. **Davan skin-light explanatory glosses Chs. 12/15/19 (Medium per Vee V3 P2).** Across three chapters, Davan's skin-light arrives with a labeling clause: *"the recognition-register, his body's way of acknowledging,"* *"the blue was the blue that came when a thing was true,"* *"responding to the proximity before he could suppress it."* **Fix:** one-pass cleanup — keep the colors, cut the explanatory clauses. Trust the reader to accumulate the meaning across appearances. Specific instances quoted in Vee V3 Part 2.

### V3-4 — Smaller persistent items (Low-Medium)

7. **Ch. 17 "three is a number that described a loss"** (Vee V3 P2) — narrator label of what Aldric's accumulated knowledge already shows. Cut sentence; preceding image is sufficient.
8. **Ch. 29 "tenderness" italicized emotional naming** (Vee V3 P3) — Aldric should not name emotion in single italicized words; the bible says he reaches for liturgical/scholarly words first. Fix: cut italics, OR have Aldric reach for a religious word first and reject it before "tenderness" arrives.
9. **D4 bio-lock paragraph "shared parenthetical" imprecision (Cass V3 P3, Minor)** — current passage says both bloodline AND Maren had "immune systems that kept the active organism out," which is accurate for bloodline but imprecise for Maren (her mechanism is native-baseline, not immune). One-clause adjustment.
10. **"The way X does Y" comparator construction overused** (Vee V3 P1 + Vee V3 P3) — both reviewers flag this as the next-most-visible house-style pattern after the V2 sweep. ~14 instances Chs. 1-10, ~9 instances Chs. 21-30. 30% reduction would be invisible and improve reading.
11. **Woodcutter's shelter timing ambiguity Ch. 8/9** (Cass V3 P1, Major) — Vane and Maren occupy the same shelter; the text doesn't secure that Vane departed before Maren arrived. One-line "She had been gone before dawn" or similar in Ch. 8 closing closes it.
12. **"Orbital not spiral" double-first-observation** (Cass V3 P1, Minor) — Aldric Ch. 3 and Davan Ch. 6 use near-identical phrasing for the same realization; bible says Davan is "first." Distinguish their registers in the prose (make Aldric's analytical, Davan's empathic) OR amend the bible note.

- **"the color the whole community had agreed to love" Ch. 1** — Vee V3 P1 still flags this as the clearest narrator-intrusion in the section. Authorial keep stands per V1/V2; honor the choice.
