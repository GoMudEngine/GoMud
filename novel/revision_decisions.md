## D1 — Coal pouch deception mechanism

**Decision:** Forearm-warmth-in-bandage is canon. The manuscript text governs.

**Rationale:** The "forearm under bandage" mechanism is what's actually on the page across multiple chapters. The bible's "coal-dark palm" is shorthand that was never written into the manuscript. Adding palm-darkening now would require inventing scenes Maren never describes (washing her palm, applying soot, etc.) and would contradict the existing physical detail (pouch placed against forearm, candle-test reads warmth not visible mark).

**Action:**
- Manuscript: no change to mechanism. Tighten Ch. 1 packing/exit sequence so the pouch's empty-vs-full state is unambiguous (currently muddled — Ch. 1 packing reads "emptied" but Ch. 5 disposes of charcoal at the first crossing; the pouch must be packed full and emptied at the crossing).
- STORY_BIBLE: replace "coal-dark palm" language with "warmed forearm under linen bandage" canonical description. Note the deacon's candle-test as a heat check, not a visual mark check.

## D2 — Bark-skin regression timing

**Decision:** Slow visible regression to begin Day 3 (Ch. 25). Days 1-2 may show internal sensation only — no flaking, no other-character observation.

**Rationale:** The "un-feeding" metaphor (per bible) requires time. A tree doesn't shed bark the morning you stop watering. The manuscript's Ch. 22 morning flaking and Ch. 23 Day-1 cuff-flake undermine the metaphor's coherence — and the metaphor is doing real thematic work (Vane's mutation as something fed, not something inflicted).

**Action:**
- Ch. 22 (post-decision morning): cut the wrist-flaking observation. Vane may notice subjective dryness or itch but no visible change.
- Ch. 23 (Day 1 east): cut Davan's observation of the flake on the cuff. Davan may register Vane's posture or scent shift but not visible bark loss.
- Ch. 24 (Day 2): minimal — a single dry-skin moment if desired, no group-visible change.
- Ch. 25 (Day 3): visible regression begins. The "wick that finally does not need to trim" lamp-tic moment is preserved; bark recession joins it as parallel.
- Ch. 26: continue the existing arc (Vane's catalog, "the grain at the inside of the elbow giving up the inside of the elbow").
- STORY_BIBLE: confirm "un-feeding metaphor requires three-day delay; visible regression starts Ch. 25, group-visible Ch. 26."

## D3 — Voiced-wall pairing mechanism

**Decision:** Hill-as-speaker is canon. The manuscript's mechanism governs.

**Rationale:** The manuscript's "the storage took a breath" / hill-as-resonator is more cinematically powerful and fits the chapter's structure (Maren outside at the recess receives the paired sense, not Aldric inside). Restricting the mechanism to the voiced wall would require either (a) Maren entering the storage during the orbital window, or (b) Aldric narrating the paired sense to a Maren who didn't experience it — both weaker.

**Action:**
- Ch. 29: add one line in Aldric's reading where he notes the storage walls and the hillside material as continuous ("the same material, of a piece, the wall as surface, the hill as body" or similar — Aldric phrasing). This grounds the Ch. 30 hill-as-speaker mechanism in Aldric's prior observation.
- Ch. 30: no change to the pairing experience.
- STORY_BIBLE: update "voiced wall" entry to "voiced hill: the storage walls and the hillside body are continuous material; the orbital response pairs at any point on the hill where the listener's disc is grounded against it."

## D4 — "Unchanged" disambiguation in Ch. 29

**Decision:** Reserve "unchanged" for the bloodline's self-description (immune-but-still-Chrysalis-modified). Use **"native-baseline"** or **"pre-organism"** for the mission-technical sense.

**Rationale:** The bloodline's *unchanged* is a self-applied identity term carrying centuries of meaning; that's the reading the reader has been trained to. The mission-records' technical term needs a different word so the bio-lock distinction holds.

**Action:**
- Ch. 29: in the bio-lock passage, replace mission-technical "unchanged" with "native-baseline" (preferred) or "pre-Chrysalis" (acceptable alternative). Keep the bloodline's self-description as "unchanged."
- Add one explicit clarifying clause: e.g., *"The mission's term was not the bloodline's term. What the records called unchanged meant the body the Chrysalis had never met. What the bloodline called unchanged meant the body the Chrysalis had met and not been able to change. The disc had been keyed to the first."*
- STORY_BIBLE: add the vocabulary distinction to Section 5.

## D5 — Davan Ch. 30 color callback

**Decision:** Add the verbatim "edge of dawn a morning nobody had seen" callback to Davan's Ch. 30 section.

**Rationale:** The Ch. 28 formulation was deliberate setup. The callback was locked in the bible. Letting it land verbatim makes the color a recurring beat the reader recognizes; substituting different language drops the planted echo without payoff.

**Action:**
- Ch. 30, Davan section: insert one beat where the color reappears, named in the same language ("the color of the edge of dawn a morning nobody had seen" or close paraphrase that preserves "morning nobody had seen").
- Surrounding prose in Davan's voice (half-thought, oblique, not over-explained).

## D6 — Disc pulse-vs-hum vocabulary

**Decision:** Manuscript is canon. Bible was wrong.

**Rationale:** Per Cass-3, the manuscript correctly establishes the disc as *pulsing* through Chs. 1-22 and shifting to continuous *hum* only Day 5 morning of Ch. 27. The bible's "first silence in three years" overstates the duration of the hum (it was pulses, not hum, for almost all of those three years).

**Action:**
- Manuscript: no change.
- STORY_BIBLE: amend "first silence in three years" to "first silence since the hum began on Day 5" or similar. The disc's three-year carry was *pulses*; the continuous *hum* begins Ch. 27.
