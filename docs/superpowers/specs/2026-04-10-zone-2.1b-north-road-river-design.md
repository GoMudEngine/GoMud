# Zone 2.1b: North Road — River Approach Design

**Date:** 2026-04-10
**Scope:** 10 new rooms extending the North Road zone northward from
room 4044 (Drainage Ditch) toward the future Stillwater zone. No
quests — exploration, NPC dialogue, and ambient mobs only.

---

## Zone Details

- **Zone:** North Road (same zone, new rooms added)
- **Folder:** `_datafiles/world/dogmud/rooms/north_road/`
- **Room IDs:** 4053–4062
- **Biome:** farmland → river valley transition
- **Connects:** 4044 (Drainage Ditch, south) → 4062 (North Road End)
- **Future:** 4062 north exit reserved for Stillwater zone (Phase 2.2)

## Room List

### 4053 — Muddy Track
The road narrows past the ditch, rutted deep by cart wheels.
Farmland gives way to rougher ground — more stone, less soil.
The sound of running water somewhere ahead.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: ruts, stones
- Exits: south → 4044, north → 4054

### 4054 — Stone Bridge
A low stone bridge spans a wide creek. The water runs fast and
brown with spring sediment. Wagon wheels have worn grooves into
the bridge stones over generations.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: bridge, creek, grooves
- Exits: south → 4053, north → 4055

### 4055 — River Road, South
The road follows the river now, terrain dropping toward the
valley floor. Willows line the bank, their branches trailing in
the current. The air is cooler and damp.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: willows, river
- Exits: south → 4054, north → 4056

### 4056 — River Ford
A shallow crossing where the road meets the river directly. Flat
stones have been laid in the riverbed to keep wagons from sinking.
The water runs ankle-deep and cold.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: stones, ford, water
- Exits: south → 4055, north → 4057
- Idle messages: water sounds, wagon rut descriptions

### 4057 — River Road, North
Past the ford, the road climbs slightly. The river curves away
eastward. The landscape opens up — you can see further here, and
the road ahead shows signs of heavier traffic.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: tracks, river
- Exits: south → 4056, north → 4058

### 4058 — Woodcutter's Track
A narrow path breaks east off the main road, marked by fresh
stumps and drag marks where logs have been hauled. The smell of
cut wood and sap.
- Biome: forest
- Nouns: stumps, drag marks, path
- Exits: south → 4057, east → 4059, north → 4060

### 4059 — Woodcutter's Camp
A rough camp among the stumps — a lean-to shelter, a chopping
block, and stacked cordwood. Someone works here regularly but
doesn't live here. A firepit holds cold ash.
- Biome: forest
- Nouns: lean-to, chopping block, cordwood, firepit
- Spawns: Woodcutter Hagen (non-combatant NPC)
- Exits: west → 4058

### 4060 — Wide Bend
The road curves west around a rocky outcrop. The land is more
open here — low hills rolling north, the river valley visible
behind to the south. A milestone reads "Stillwater — 2 leagues."
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: outcrop, milestone
- Exits: south → 4058, north → 4061

### 4061 — Travelers' Rest
A flat area beside the road where wagons have pulled off to camp.
Old firepits, a crude hitching post, flattened grass. A traveler
sits on a rock, watching the road north.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: firepits, hitching post, rock
- Spawns: Lone traveler (non-combatant NPC)
- Exits: south → 4060, north → 4062

### 4062 — North Road End
The road widens and the terrain flattens. Ahead, the first signs
of a settlement — smoke on the horizon, the distant sound of
hammering, the smell of fish and cookfires. The road to Stillwater
begins here.
- Biome: farmland
- Nouns: smoke, horizon, road
- Exits: south → 4061 (future: north → Stillwater)

## NPCs

### Woodcutter Hagen
- **Location:** Room 4059 (Woodcutter's Camp)
- **Type:** non-combatant, charm-immune
- **Dialogue topics:** his work, the road north, Stillwater
  rumors, recent weather, bloodline agents seen on the road
- **Idle:** chops wood, stacks logs, wipes brow
- **Purpose:** world-building, Stillwater teaser, lore

### Lone Traveler
- **Location:** Room 4061 (Travelers' Rest)
- **Type:** non-combatant, charm-immune
- **Dialogue topics:** Stillwater description, dangers on the
  road, bloodline agents asking questions about "a girl who
  traveled north," trade opportunities
- **Idle:** watches the road, adjusts pack, eats dried meat
- **Purpose:** quest foreshadowing (Maren's trail), Stillwater
  preview, world feels inhabited

## Hostile Mobs

### River Rats
- **Locations:** Rooms 4055, 4056 (river rooms)
- **Type:** hostile, low-level vermin, groups of 2
- **Archetype:** fighting, low statpool (~40)
- **Drops:** nothing meaningful (trash mob)
- **Behavior:** territorial near the water

### Wild Dogs
- **Locations:** Rooms 4053, 4057, 4060 (road rooms, wandering)
- **Type:** hostile, pack animal
- **Archetype:** fighting, moderate statpool (~60)
- **MaxWander:** 2
- **Behavior:** roam in pairs, slightly tougher than rats
- **Drops:** nothing meaningful

## Items

No new items required. Existing world items and mob drops
are sufficient. The woodcutter's camp has flavor nouns but no
lootable containers — this stretch is about travel, not
treasure.
