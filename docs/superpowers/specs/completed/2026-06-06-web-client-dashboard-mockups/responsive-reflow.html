Phone / very-narrow behavior — pick one:
▾

Bottom tab-bar + drawer (recommended)

Feed stays full-screen and usable; a bottom icon bar (map / vitals / status / chat / art / triggers) slides a panel up over the feed on tap. Best for actually playing on a phone.

≣

Single stacked scroll

Everything stacks in one column (feed on top, command, then panels below) and you scroll the whole page. Simpler, but you scroll a lot and the feed competes with panels for space.
