# Zone 1.2: Ashwick — Design Spec

*Maren's home hamlet. A quiet place with a secret.*

---

## Overview

**Zone:** Ashwick
**Zone folder:** `ashwick`
**Room IDs:** 4015-4034 (20 rooms)
**Mob IDs:** 259-267 (9 mobs)
**Quest IDs:** 16 (Herbalist's Shortage), 17 (The Empty Cottage)
**Item IDs:** 40040 (forest herbs), 40041 (father's letter), 40042 (recipe page)
**Biome:** `farmland` (hamlet rooms), `forest` (outskirts/woods)
**Difficulty:** Safe (hamlet), low (forest edge), mid (deep woods)
**Connection:** East from Ashwick Crossroads room 4014 (-8, -13)
**Purpose:** Maren's home hamlet from the novel. Layered discovery —
surface is an ordinary farming village, deeper exploration reveals the
story of "the girl who left" and connects to the novel's central mystery.

## Room Details

### Stage 1.2a — Hamlet Proper

#### 4015 — Village Gate
- **Coords:** (-7, -13, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** W→4014 (zone: Marches Spur Road), E→4016, N→4026, S→4025
- **Theme:** Entry to Ashwick. A low stone wall with a wooden gate
  that hasn't been closed in years. The hamlet is visible ahead —
  small, tidy, unremarkable. A faded sign: "Ashwick."
- **Nouns:** gate (wooden, weathered, hinges rusted open — more
  boundary marker than barrier), sign (plain wood, "Ashwick" in
  faded paint, below it "est." and a date too worn to read)
- **Idle:** Wind through the gate, a dog barking somewhere inside

#### 4016 — Hamlet Road
- **Coords:** (-6, -13, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** W→4015, E→4017, S→4020
- **Theme:** The main (only) road through Ashwick. Packed earth,
  a few houses on either side, the smell of cooking fires and
  animals. Quiet, lived-in, the kind of place where nothing
  happens and everyone knows it.
- **Nouns:** houses (small fieldstone-and-thatch, well-maintained,
  kitchen gardens behind low walls), road (packed earth, smooth
  from generations of feet, a single set of cart ruts)
- **Spawn:** mobid 266 (village chicken)

#### 4017 — Central Green
- **Coords:** (-5, -13, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** W→4016, E→4018, S→4021, N→4027
- **Theme:** The hamlet's heart. A patch of green with a bench,
  a notice post, and a spreading oak. People gather here.
  Children play. The ritual circle is visible to the south.
- **Nouns:** oak (ancient, spreading, the kind of tree that was
  here before the hamlet and will be here after), bench (worn
  smooth, carved initials, the social center of the village),
  notice post (a few posted notices — a lost goat, a market day
  reminder, and one older notice about "reporting unusual
  absences to the deacon")
- **Spawn:** mobid 266 (village chicken)
- **Idle:** Children's voices from a nearby yard, leaves rustling

#### 4018 — Chapel Lane
- **Coords:** (-4, -13, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** W→4017, E→4019, S→4022
- **Theme:** The lane leading to the deacon's chapel. Quieter than
  the green. A few houses with closed shutters. The chapel's
  modest spire is visible above the roofline.
- **Nouns:** lane (narrower than the main road, better maintained
  — someone sweeps it), shutters (closed on several houses, the
  particular stillness of homes whose occupants left or died)
- **Spawn:** mobid 266 (village chicken)

#### 4019 — East Edge
- **Coords:** (-3, -13, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** W→4018, N→4029, S→4023
- **Theme:** Where the hamlet ends and the forest begins. The last
  house is behind you. Ahead, the tree line. The transition is
  abrupt — farmland stops, forest starts, as if an agreement
  was made about where each one's territory ends.
- **Nouns:** treeline (dense mixed woodland beginning just past
  the last fence post, the canopy dark and close), fence (the
  last fence in Ashwick, marking the boundary between tended
  land and what isn't)
- **Idle:** Bird calls from the forest, the creak of the last fence

#### 4020 — Farmstead
- **Coords:** (-6, -14, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** N→4016
- **Theme:** A working farm. Fenced paddock, a barn, root
  vegetables in rows. Farmer Hesta lives here. The farm is
  productive but the farmer looks tired.
- **Nouns:** paddock (a few goats, a draft horse with a patient
  expression), barn (small, functional, tools hung on pegs
  inside the open door), crops (root vegetables in neat rows,
  recently weeded — someone works this land alone)
- **Spawn:** mobid 261 (Farmer Hesta)
- **Idle:** A goat bleats, tools clink in the barn

#### 4021 — Ritual Circle
- **Coords:** (-5, -14, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** N→4017
- **Theme:** The Autumnal Rite site. A circle of flat stones in
  the grass, large enough for the hamlet to gather. Flower
  garlands from the last Rite are drying on poles. This is where
  the community celebrates its Bloomings — and where Maren's
  deception was nearly exposed.
- **Nouns:** stones (flat river stones set in a circle, each
  worn smooth by generations of standing feet; one stone near
  the east edge has a dark <ansi fg="itemname">scorch mark</ansi>
  that doesn't match fire damage — something else burned here),
  scorch mark (a dark discoloration on the stone's surface, not
  soot — more like the stone itself changed color. It is roughly
  hand-shaped, fingers splayed, as if someone pressed a burning
  palm to the rock. The surrounding stones are unmarked. Novel
  breadcrumb — the coal pouch that almost exposed Maren),
  garlands (dried flower garlands on wooden poles, the remains
  of the last Autumnal Rite — chrysanthemums, sage bundles, and
  strips of dyed cloth in the Chrysalis colors)
- **Idle:** Wind stirs the drying garlands, a petal falls

#### 4022 — Deacon's Chapel
- **Coords:** (-4, -14, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** N→4018
- **Theme:** A modest stone chapel, well-kept. The Chrysalis
  symbol above the door. Ferris tends this place with the quiet
  pride of a man whose congregation is small but genuine.
- **Nouns:** symbol (the Chrysalis spiral above the door, carved
  in stone — but look closely and the oldest layer of the
  carving, beneath the spiral, has a different geometry; the
  inner orbit, partially obscured by the later work; novel
  breadcrumb), door (heavy oak, polished handle, always
  unlocked), pews (six wooden pews, enough for the hamlet;
  prayer cushions worn thin at the knees)
- **Spawn:** mobid 260 (Deacon Ferris)
- **Idle:** Candle flame flickers, the smell of incense

#### 4026 — Old Well
- **Coords:** (-7, -12, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** S→4015
- **Theme:** The hamlet's well, stone-ringed, with a wooden winch.
  Still used. The stones around the base are carved with old
  marks — blessings for clean water, most of them. One is
  different.
- **Nouns:** well (stone-ringed, deep, the water clean and cold;
  the winch rope is frayed but holds), <ansi fg="itemname">marks</ansi>
  (most of the carvings around the well's base are water
  blessings in the standard Chrysalis iconography. One mark,
  older than the rest, partially overgrown with moss, is not a
  blessing. It is the inner orbit symbol — the same geometry as
  the disc edge, the temple doorway at the Confluence, the
  margin notation on old maps. Someone carved it here a long
  time ago. Novel breadcrumb — discoverable by curious players,
  not explained)
- **Idle:** The well rope creaks in the wind, water echoes below

#### 4027 — Delia's Cottage
- **Coords:** (-5, -12, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** S→4017, E→4028
- **Theme:** A tidy cottage with herb bundles hanging from every
  beam. The smell is extraordinary — sage, lavender, fennel,
  things you can't name. Delia lives and works here. A sign on
  the door reads "Limited stock — check back later."
- **Nouns:** herbs (bundles of dried herbs hanging from ceiling
  beams — sage, feverfew, comfrey, things with names you'd need
  training to know; several hooks are empty where bundles have
  been taken and not replaced), sign (hand-lettered on a wood
  scrap: "Limited stock — check back later." The letters are
  neat and slightly annoyed. Quest breadcrumb #2), worktable
  (a scarred wooden table with a mortar and pestle, cutting
  board, and several glass jars — some empty, some containing
  preparations in various stages. The organized workspace of
  someone who has not been able to work at full capacity)
- **Spawn:** mobid 259 (Delia)
- **Idle:** Dried herbs rustle in a draft, mortar scrapes on stone

### Stage 1.2b — Outskirts

#### 4028 — Delia's Garden
- **Coords:** (-4, -12, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** W→4027
- **Theme:** A cultivated herb garden behind the cottage. Raised
  beds, a cold frame, careful labeling. Some beds are thriving.
  Others are bare — harvested and not replanted.
- **Nouns:** beds (raised wooden beds with hand-written labels;
  the comfrey and sage beds are healthy; the feverfew and
  bitter-thistle beds are stripped bare), frame (a glass cold
  frame protecting seedlings — Delia is trying to grow replacements
  but they're weeks from harvest)
- **Idle:** Bees working the remaining herbs, wind through the beds

#### 4023 — Maren's Cottage
- **Coords:** (-3, -14, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** N→4019, S→4024
- **Theme:** An abandoned cottage at the hamlet's southeast edge.
  A faded "for sale" sign nailed to the door. The windows are
  shuttered. The garden has gone wild. The silence here is
  specific — not empty but vacated, the quiet of a place someone
  left deliberately.
- **Nouns:** sign (a hand-painted "for sale" sign, the paint
  faded, nailed crookedly to the door frame — it has been here
  long enough that no one expects it to work; quest breadcrumb
  #1 for The Empty Cottage), door (unlocked — the latch lifts
  easily, as if someone left it that way on purpose), windows
  (shuttered from inside, a gap in one shutter lets a thin line
  of light into the interior)
- **Interior detail nouns (inside the cottage):**
  shelf (a row of shelves against the wall, mostly bare; a gap
  where books were removed, dust outlines showing their size;
  one remaining book, a pressed flower between its pages — dried
  fennel, the kind Delia grows),
  hearth (cold, unused for years; the soot pattern on the back
  wall suggests someone sat here for hours, close, as if seeking
  warmth from a fire that wasn't lit; a faint smell of lamp oil
  lingers — or you imagine it does),
  <ansi fg="itemname">stone</ansi> (a loose stone near the base
  of the hearth, slightly displaced from the others; it moves
  when pressed — behind it, a small cavity in the wall),
  <ansi fg="itemname">drawer</ansi> (a drawer in a small bedside
  table, swollen with damp and stiff to open; inside, a folded
  page of careful handwriting)
- **Containers:**
  stone:
    items:
      - itemid: 40041
  drawer:
    items:
      - itemid: 40042
- **Spawn:** mobid 267 (cottage mouse)
- **Idle:** A mouse scurries across the floor, dust motes in the
  light from the shutter gap, the smell of old wood and absence

#### 4024 — Cottage Garden
- **Coords:** (-3, -15, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** N→4023
- **Theme:** The garden behind Maren's cottage, gone wild. What
  was once a kitchen garden is now a tangle of self-seeded herbs
  and weeds fighting for space. Delia's training is visible in
  what was planted — the layout was good. The neglect is visible
  in what it's become.
- **Nouns:** garden (a kitchen garden overwhelmed by three years
  of neglect; the raised beds are still visible under the weeds,
  the layout orderly — someone who knew herbs planned this),
  herbs (self-seeded comfrey, wild sage, and fennel growing
  among the weeds — still alive, still useful to someone who
  knows what they're looking at)
- **Idle:** Insects drone in the overgrowth, a bird lands on a
  fence post and departs

#### 4025 — South Fields
- **Coords:** (-7, -14, 0) | **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** N→4015
- **Theme:** Fallow fields south of the village gate. Open sky,
  the edge of what Ashwick tends. The land is flat and quiet.
- **Nouns:** fields (fallow, plowed but not planted this season;
  the soil is dark and rich but no one is working it), boundary
  (a line of stones marking the village's southern limit — beyond
  it, wilder grass and the road south)
- **Idle:** Wind across the open fields, a crow calls

#### 4029 — Forest Path
- **Coords:** (-3, -12, 0) | **Biome:** forest
- **Exits:** S→4019, N→4030
- **Theme:** The path from Ashwick into the woods. Narrow, well-
  worn by foragers and herb-gatherers. The canopy closes overhead.
  The light changes from farmland gold to forest green. The air
  is cooler and smells of leaf mold and damp earth.
- **Nouns:** path (narrow but clear, beaten by years of foot
  traffic; herb-gatherers use this regularly), canopy (mixed
  hardwood and evergreen, the branches interlocking overhead
  to filter the light into green-gold dapples)
- **Spawn:** mobid 263 (forest fox)
- **Idle:** Birdsong, branches creaking, light shifting through
  leaves

#### 4030 — Deep Woods
- **Coords:** (-3, -11, 0) | **Biome:** forest
- **Exits:** S→4029, N→4031
- **Theme:** Deeper into the forest. The path narrows further.
  Undergrowth thickens. Less light. The sounds of the hamlet
  are completely gone — just forest sounds now. Something has
  been through here recently, breaking small branches at
  waist height.
- **Nouns:** undergrowth (dense ferns and bramble, the kind of
  growth that closes a path if it isn't walked regularly; this
  path is walked regularly), breaks (small branches snapped at
  waist height along the path — someone has been pushing through
  here carrying something bulky, like bundles of herbs)
- **Spawn:** mobid 263 (forest fox), mobid 265 (briar hawk)
- **Idle:** Something moves in the undergrowth, a hawk cries above

#### 4031 — Forager's Camp
- **Coords:** (-3, -10, 0) | **Biome:** forest
- **Exits:** S→4030, W→4032
- **Theme:** A hidden camp in a forest clearing. A lean-to of
  branches and canvas, a cold firepit, bundles of dried herbs
  stacked neatly against a tree. Someone has been living here
  for weeks — quietly, tidily, the camp of a person who knows
  how to be invisible.
- **Nouns:** lean-to (branches and salvaged canvas, well-built
  for something temporary — the work of someone who has slept
  rough before and learned from it), firepit (cold, deliberately
  so — no smoke to draw attention; the ash is scattered and
  buried after each use), herbs (bundles of dried herbs, neatly
  tied and stacked — feverfew, bitter-thistle, comfrey; the same
  herbs missing from Delia's beds)
- **Spawn:** mobid 262 (The Forager)
- **Idle:** A twig snaps in the clearing, the camp is very still

#### 4032 — Herb Clearing
- **Coords:** (-4, -10, 0) | **Biome:** forest
- **Exits:** E→4031, N→4033
- **Theme:** A natural clearing where sunlight reaches the forest
  floor. Rich herb growth — the kind of place an herbalist would
  prize. Signs of recent harvesting, but also signs of regrowth.
  The herbs are being taken but not destroyed.
- **Nouns:** herbs (dense growth of medicinal herbs — feverfew,
  bitter-thistle, healers-root — in the sunlit clearing; some
  patches have been harvested recently with clean cuts, not
  torn; whoever is taking them knows what they're doing),
  clearing (a natural gap in the canopy where a tree fell years
  ago; the light feeds a patch of herb growth richer than
  anything in Delia's garden)
- **Spawn:** mobid 265 (briar hawk)
- **Idle:** Sunlight shifts across the clearing, insects hum

#### 4033 — Hidden Grove
- **Coords:** (-4, -9, 0) | **Biome:** forest
- **Exits:** S→4032, N→4034
- **Theme:** A deeper grove, less visited. The herbs here are
  untouched — the forager hasn't found this spot. Older growth,
  some rare varieties. The air has a quality that suggests the
  Chrysalis is stronger here — the plants are slightly wrong,
  slightly more vivid, slightly more alive than they should be.
- **Nouns:** herbs (a dense patch of rare medicinal plants —
  including varieties Delia would recognize but not expect to
  find this close to a hamlet; the Chrysalis influence on the
  soil has produced something unusual here), growth (the plants
  here are subtly wrong — colors too vivid, leaves with faint
  bioluminescence at the edges, stems that seem to lean toward
  you; the Chrysalis is active in this soil)
- **Containers:**
  herbs:
    items:
      - itemid: 40040
- **Spawn:** mobid 264 (timber wolf)
- **Idle:** Faint luminescence at the edge of vision, unnatural
  stillness, a low hum from the soil

#### 4034 — Overgrown Hollow
- **Coords:** (-4, -8, 0) | **Biome:** forest
- **Exits:** S→4033
- **Theme:** The deepest point of the forest accessible from
  Ashwick. A hollow between old roots where the canopy is
  almost complete. Dark, damp, the feel of a place that is
  not hostile but is not meant for people. The wolves den
  nearby. This is as far as anyone goes.
- **Nouns:** hollow (a depression between the roots of an
  enormous old tree, carpeted with moss and old leaves; the
  air is cool and still; animal tracks — wolf — circle the
  hollow but don't enter it, as if the spot has an agreement
  with them), roots (massive, half-exposed, the tree above
  them old enough that its roots have reshaped the terrain;
  claw marks on the bark suggest wolves use this as a marking
  post)
- **Spawn:** mobid 264 (timber wolf)
- **Idle:** The hollow breathes cool air, wolf tracks in the mud

## Quest 16: The Herbalist's Shortage

### Quest Chain
```
start → (resolve herb supply) → end
```

### Breadcrumbs (3)
1. **Delia** (4027) — `ask delia quest` or `ask delia task`
2. **Delia's door sign** — visible in room 4027 description:
   "Limited stock — check back later"
3. **Farmer Hesta** (4020) — `ask hesta herbs` or general dialogue
   mentions the shortage

### Quest Item
- **Forest Herbs** (40040) — found in `herbs` container in room
  4033 (Hidden Grove). Player `get herbs` from container.

### Resolution Path A — Negotiate with the Forager
1. Find the forager's camp (4031) — the broken branches in the
   deep woods (4030) are the trail
2. Talk to the forager. Initial dialogue is defensive.
3. Ask about herbs/why — learn they're hollow, trading herbs to
   survive
4. Ask about negotiate/work/delia — requires having spoken to
   Delia first (questRequired: 16-start). The forager agrees
   to work with Delia if the player vouches for them.
5. Return to Delia — `ask delia forager` or `ask delia help`
   (while quest active) completes the quest. The forager gets
   a supply arrangement, Delia gets her herbs.

### Resolution Path B — Find Alternate Source
1. Explore past the forager's camp to the herb clearing (4032)
   and hidden grove (4033)
2. Get forest herbs from the container in room 4033
3. Return to Delia — `give herbs delia` OR `ask delia herbs`
   while holding them completes the quest
4. The forager remains in the woods — unresolved but Delia's
   supply is fixed from the new source

### Elephant Path Coverage
- `give herbs delia` — onGive script handles quest completion
- `ask delia herbs` while holding herbs — dialogue requiresItem
  node handles completion
- `ask delia quest` after completion — questExcluded prevents
  re-trigger
- Player finds herbs before talking to Delia — onGive grants
  both start and end (same pattern as Malk)
- Player tries to give non-herb items to Delia — onGive returns
  them with a polite refusal

### Rewards
- Gold (modest)
- Delia offers foraging skill training (unlocks her as a trainer)
- Delia becomes a persistent herb/potion vendor
- If Path A: The Forager moves from the camp to a spot near
  Delia's garden (but per the Malk precedent — Forager stays
  at camp, dialogue acknowledges the arrangement was made)

## Quest 17: The Empty Cottage

### Quest Chain
```
No chain — pure discovery. Finding each item grants an
independent quest token.
```

### Breadcrumbs (3)
1. **"For sale" sign** on Maren's Cottage (4023) — visible in
   room description
2. **Farmer Hesta** (4020) — mentions "the family that left"
   and the cottage
3. **Old Well marks** (4026) — the inner orbit symbol carved
   in the stone; curious players may investigate further

### Discovery Items
- **Father's Letter** (40041) — in `stone` container, room 4023.
  Noun `stone` is ANSI-highlighted. Grants quest token "17-letter".
  Seeds future NP quest (mentions "Voss in New Plymouth").
- **Recipe Page** (40042) — in `drawer` container, room 4023.
  Noun `drawer` is ANSI-highlighted. Grants quest token
  "17-recipe". Triggers recipe/skill discovery on pickup.

### No Completion State
Both items can be found independently. There is no "turn in"
step. The letter and recipe are their own rewards — the letter
for lore-hunters, the recipe for practical players. Both items
persist in inventory as keepsakes/references.

### Room Script (4023.js)
An `onCommand` script handles the quest token grants when
players interact with the containers:
- Taking the letter: grants "17-letter" token, sends flavor text
  about finding the hidden cavity
- Taking the recipe: grants "17-recipe" token, triggers skill
  discovery, sends flavor text about the student's handwriting
