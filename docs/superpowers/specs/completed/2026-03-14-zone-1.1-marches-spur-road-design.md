# Zone 1.1: Marches Spur Road — Design Spec

*The road from Watchers Crossing south to the Ashwick Crossroads.*

---

## Overview

**Zone:** Marches Spur Road
**Zone folder:** `marches_spur_road`
**Room IDs:** 4000-4014 (15 rooms)
**Biome:** `plains` (4000-4005), `farmland` (4006-4014)
**Difficulty:** Mid-tier throughout (comparable to Thornwall Outskirts)
**Connection:** Forks south from Watchers Crossing room 425 (River Bank)
**Mob IDs:** 250-258 (see NPC section for assignments)
**Purpose:** Bridge existing backwater content (Sanctum Basin / Thornwall)
to the novel's main geography. First road into the wider Windward Marches.

## Mini-Stages

### Stage 1.1a — The Road South (rooms 4000-4009)

10 rooms. The fork from Watchers Crossing heading south through scrubland
that transitions to farmland. Contains the peddler quest giver, the
waypoint shrine, and the ruined barn (bandit camp / quest resolution site).

### Stage 1.1b — The Inn and Crossroads (rooms 4010-4014)

5 rooms. The approach to the waypoint inn and the Ashwick Crossroads
junction. Contains the inn (social hub, gossiper NPCs), and the crossroads
room that anchors all future Phase 1-2 zone connections.

## Room Details

### 4000 — Spur Road, Southern Fork
- **Coords:** (-8, -2, 0)
- **Biome:** plains
- **Exits:** north → 425 (Watchers Crossing, River Bank), south → 4001
- **Description:** The fork. A rougher track branches south from the river
  crossing. A weathered signpost lists destinations: Ashwick, Stillwater,
  the southern Marches. The road is narrower than the east-west route,
  the ruts shallower — less traffic, but traffic nonetheless.
- **Nouns:** signpost (lists destinations south — Ashwick, Stillwater,
  Amber Valley; the paint is faded but legible), ruts (wagon ruts, less
  frequent than the main road but present; someone uses this route)
- **Idle messages:** Wind-driven dust, the sound of the river fading behind

### 4001 — Scrubland Bend
- **Coords:** (-8, -3, 0)
- **Biome:** plains
- **Exits:** north → 4000, south → 4002
- **Description:** The road bends through dry scrub. Sage and bunchgrass.
  The river sound is gone; the wind is the dominant noise. A lizard
  watches from a rock.
- **Nouns:** scrub (sage and dry grass), rock (sun-warmed basalt, lizard
  on top)
- **Spawn:** scrub coyote (low-mid, non-aggro unless provoked)

### 4002 — Waypoint Shrine
- **Coords:** (-8, -4, 0)
- **Biome:** plains
- **Exits:** north → 4001, west → 4003, south → 4004
- **Description:** A small stone shrine beside the road, knee-high, worn
  smooth by hands and weather. Offerings of dried flowers and road-bread
  are tucked into its crevices. The tradition is old — leave something
  for safe passage, take nothing.
- **Nouns:** shrine (worn stone, carved with a simple blessing; the
  carving style is old, predating the current Chrysalis iconography),
  offerings (dried flowers, road-bread, a few coins green with age;
  one recent addition: a carved wooden prayer asking for safe passage
  for freight wagons on the south road — quest breadcrumb #2)
- **Idle messages:** A dry breeze stirs the dried flowers at the shrine

### 4003 — Peddler's Camp
- **Coords:** (-9, -4, 0)
- **Biome:** plains
- **Exits:** east → 4002
- **Description:** A cleared patch off the road where a canvas lean-to
  shelters a modest camp. A firepit, a cart with one wheel propped on
  a stone, crates and bundles organized with the precision of someone
  who lives from their inventory. The camp has the settled look of
  someone who has been here longer than they planned.
- **Nouns:** cart (a peddler's cart, well-maintained but not moving;
  one wheel is propped, not broken — he's waiting, not stuck), firepit
  (recently used, coals still warm), crates (trade goods — rope, oil,
  dried provisions, basic tools; the kind of stock a road peddler
  carries between small towns)
- **Spawn:** Peddler Malk (quest giver + vendor)
- **Idle messages:** Smoke from the camp fire, the peddler checking his
  inventory, wind flapping the canvas

### 4004 — Open Country
- **Coords:** (-8, -5, 0)
- **Biome:** plains
- **Exits:** north → 4002, south → 4005
- **Description:** The scrub thins. The land flattens and opens up —
  long views in every direction, the sky enormous. To the south,
  the first suggestion of tilled earth. The road is straight here,
  a pale line across open grassland.
- **Nouns:** grassland (tall dry grass, seed-heavy, bending in the
  wind), horizon (long views — to the north, the crossing's bridge

- **Spawn:** field sparrow (non-hostile), scrub coyote

### 4005 — Old Fence Line
- **Coords:** (-8, -6, 0)
- **Biome:** plains
- **Exits:** north → 4004, south → 4006
- **Description:** Rotting fence posts line both sides of the road,
  the wire long gone, the posts leaning at angles that suggest
  decades of neglect. Someone claimed this land once and gave up on
  it, or left, or died. The grass grows taller here, fed by whatever
  the fence once enclosed.
- **Nouns:** posts (old hardwood, gray and split, leaning but not

- **Spawn:** road bandit (mid-tier, hostile)

### 4006 — Farmland Transition

- **Exits:** north → 4005, west → 4007, south → 4008
- **Description:** The scrub is gone. The road passes between fallow

- **Nouns:** fields (fallow but recently tilled; someone is working

### 4007 — Ruined Barn
- **Coords:** (-9, -7, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** east → 4006
- **Description:** A barn that lost its argument with time. Half the

- **Spawn:** road bandit x2 (mid-tier, hostile; one is the "leader"

### 4008 — Wagon Ruts
- **Coords:** (-8, -8, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** north → 4006, south → 4009
- **Description:** The road widens and the ruts deepen — heavy wagons
  use this stretch regularly. The farmland on both sides is active
  now, not fallow: crops in rows, irrigation ditches, the particular
  orderliness of land someone depends on. The road is better
  maintained from here south.
- **Nouns:** ruts (deep, wide-set; heavy freight wagons, not farm
  carts), crops (low green rows, recently tended; the farming here
  is organized and competent)
- **Spawn:** farm cat (non-hostile)

### 4009 — Ridge View
- **Coords:** (-8, -9, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** north → 4008, south → 4010
- **Description:** A gentle rise in the road affords a view south.

- **Nouns:** smoke (chimney smoke from the inn, steady — the fire

### 4010 — Descending Track
- **Coords:** (-8, -10, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** north → 4009, south → 4011
- **Description:** The road descends toward the inn. A wagon passes
  heading north, the driver nodding without stopping. The traffic
  is heavier here — this stretch serves the crossroads, and the
  crossroads serves the Marches.
- **Nouns:** road (well-packed, wide enough for two wagons to pass;
  the surface is graveled in patches, someone's investment in

- **Spawn:** Traveler Bren (non-hostile, wanders 4009-4011, gossiper)

### 4011 — Waypoint Inn Yard
- **Coords:** (-8, -11, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** north → 4010, west → 4012, south → 4013
- **Description:** The inn yard: packed earth, a hitching post with
  two horses tied, a water trough, a stack of firewood against the
  wall. A notice board beside the door is pinned with curling papers.
  The inn itself is a solid two-story building of fieldstone and
  timber, its sign depicting a broken yoke with the halves pointing
  in different directions.
- **Nouns:** sign (a painted wooden sign: a yoke split cleanly in
  half, the two halves pointing opposite ways; "The Broken Yoke"
  in plain letters beneath), notice board (job postings, road
  condition warnings, a faded bounty notice, and a hand-written
  note asking if anyone has seen a freight shipment from the north
  — quest breadcrumb #3 via Thessa), horses (two draft horses,
  patient and disinterested), trough (water trough, clean, recently
  filled)

### 4012 — The Broken Yoke Inn
- **Coords:** (-9, -11, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** east → 4011
- **Description:** A common room built for function over style. A
  stone fireplace dominates the south wall, the fire low and steady.
  Trestle tables fill the room, scarred by years of use. The bar is
  a plank on barrels, honest about what it is. The smell is wood
  smoke and bread and the particular warmth of a room where people
  stop moving for a while. A few travelers sit at the tables, eating
  or talking or simply being somewhere with a roof.
- **Nouns:** fireplace (fieldstone, soot-blackened, the fire kept
  burning day and night; the mantel holds a clay jug and a carved
  figure of a draft horse), bar (a plank on barrels; behind it,
  shelves of bottles, a bread board, a soup pot kept warm on a
  trivet), tables (trestle tables, each one carved and stained
  differently; the history of the inn is written in its furniture)
- **Spawn:** Innkeeper Thessa (vendor + gossiper), 1-2 ambient
  patron NPCs (gossiper group, idle behaviors: eating, drinking,
  muttering)
- **Idle messages:** The fire crackles, a patron coughs, the clink
  of cups, the innkeeper wipes down the bar

### 4013 — South Road Approach
- **Coords:** (-8, -12, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** north → 4011, south → 4014
- **Description:** The road south of the inn, approaching the
  crossroads. A farmer's cart passes loaded with root vegetables.
  The traffic here comes from all directions — the crossroads
  pulls it in and sorts it out.
- **Nouns:** cart (a farmer's cart, the produce muddy and fresh;
  the farmer glances at you without interest — just another
  traveler)
- **Spawn:** field sparrow (non-hostile)

### 4014 — Ashwick Crossroads
- **Coords:** (-8, -13, 0)
- **Biome:** farmland
- **Exits:** north → 4013, south → (future: Amber Valley road),
  east → (future: Ashwick / Sanctum Basin spur),
  southwest → (future: Stillwater / North Road)
- **Description:** Three roads meet at a junction marked by a
  cluster of signposts. North: "Watchers Crossing, Thornwall."
  South: "Amber Valley, the Confluence." East: "Ashwick, the
  Basin retreats." Southwest: "Stillwater, New Plymouth." The
  crossroads is a natural pause — a place where you decide which
  direction the rest of your story goes. The ground is packed
  hard by a thousand choices.
- **Nouns:** signposts (weathered wood, each post pointing a
  different direction; the lettering is plain and practical,
  distance markers in days of travel rather than miles; the
  New Plymouth post has been repainted more recently than the
  others), junction (the roads converge on a roughly circular
  patch of packed earth; wheel ruts from every direction overlap
  into a palimpsest of journeys)
- **Idle messages:** Wind stirs dust at the crossroads, a bird
  perches on a signpost, distant wagon sounds from the south

### Bandit Leader (Dialogue-capable variant)
- **Mob ID:** 254
- **Location:** Room 4007 only (Ruined Barn)
- **Groups:** humanoid, bandit
- **Species ID:** 1
- **Hostile:** false (aggro is scripted, not innate)
- **Archetype:** fighting
- **Stat pool:** 75
- **Drops:** Gold, stolen goods, a crude weapon
- **Aggro mechanism:** The leader spawns as `hostile: false` so the
  player can initiate dialogue first. An `onIdle` or `onPlayerEnter`
  script starts a countdown (3 rounds). If the player doesn't engage
  in dialogue within 3 rounds, the script calls `mob.Command('attack')`
  to initiate combat. If the player talks first (`ask bandit toll` etc.),
  the countdown is cancelled and the dialogue tree handles resolution.
  This mimics the "talk fast or fight" dynamic without requiring engine
  changes.
- **Dialogue (room 4007 only):**
  - "toll" / "deal" / "negotiate": Offers two sub-paths:
    - Pay gold toll (50 gold) → releases freight, no combat
    - Rhetoric check via opposed roll (player Charisma vs leader
      Willpower). On success: releases freight, bandits de-aggro
      for this player. On failure: "Nice try. Pay the toll or we
      do this the hard way." — player can still pay or fight.
      Rhetoric check can be re-attempted once per visit.

## Quest: The Peddler's Overdue Freight

### Quest ID
TBD (next available quest number)

### Quest Chain
```
start → (find crate) → complete
```

### Breadcrumbs (3 independent discovery paths)
1. **Peddler Malk** (4003) — `ask malk quest` or `ask malk task`
2. **Waypoint Shrine** (4002) — `look offerings` reveals carved prayer
   about freight wagons
3. **Innkeeper Thessa** (4012) — `ask thessa road` or `ask thessa trouble`
   mentions the peddler's problem

### Quest Item
- **Freight Crate** — found in the `crates` container in room 4007
  (Ruined Barn). Player must `get crate` or `take crate` from the
  container. Item description references road freight markings matching
  Malk's trade goods.

### Resolution Path A — Combat
1. Go to Ruined Barn (4007)
2. Fight the bandits (2 road bandits including leader)
3. Take freight crate from the crates container
4. Return to Malk at camp (4003)
5. Complete via EITHER:
   - `ask malk freight` / `ask malk crate` / `ask malk shipment`
     (while holding the crate) — dialogue node with `requiresItem`
     advances quest
   - `give crate malk` — onGive script detects quest item, advances
     quest identically

### Resolution Path B — Negotiate
1. Go to Ruined Barn (4007)
2. Talk to bandit leader BEFORE attacking: `ask bandit toll` or
   `ask bandit deal` or `ask bandit negotiate`
3. Sub-path B1: Pay gold toll → leader releases crate, combat avoided
4. Sub-path B2: Rhetoric check → convince leader, crate released,
   bandits de-aggro permanently for this player
5. Take freight crate from container (now accessible without combat)
6. Return to Malk — same two completion methods as Path A

### Elephant Path Coverage
- `give crate malk` — handled by onGive script (CRITICAL per give.go
  SOP: item transfers before script fires; script detects quest item
  and advances quest)
- `ask malk crate/freight/shipment` while holding item — dialogue
  node with `requiresItem` field
- `ask malk quest` after already completing — `questExcluded` prevents
  re-trigger, Malk says "You already helped me out. I won't forget it."
- Player loses the crate — crate respawns in the barn container on
  room reset. Malk has a dialogue node: "Lost the shipment? Should
  still be at that barn if no one's moved it."
- Player attacks bandit leader mid-dialogue — combat starts normally,
  falls back to Path A
- Player tries to give a non-quest item to Malk — onGive returns it:
  "I appreciate the thought, but that's yours."

### Rewards
- Gold payment (modest)
- Malk's vendor prices reduced 10% permanently (per-player flag)
- Malk dialogue updates to acknowledge completion

### Quest Gating
- `grantsQuest` on completion node has `questExcluded` to prevent
  double completion
- No `expiryPeriod` (Malk waits indefinitely — he's patient)
- `questRequired` used for gated dialogue, not `requires`

## Novel Flavor Integration

This zone is not a novel location — it's infrastructure connecting the
backwater to the novel's world. The novel flavor comes through NPC
dialogue, not room descriptions:

- **Malk** references New Plymouth, the docks district, bloodline patrols
- **Thessa** relays gossip from across the Marches via the gossiper system
- **Bren** mentions Stillwater and bloodline agents asking questions
- **The signpost at 4014** names the novel's locations as destinations

No quest content references the novel's plot directly. The zone's job
is to make the player aware that a larger world exists and give them
a reason to head toward it.
