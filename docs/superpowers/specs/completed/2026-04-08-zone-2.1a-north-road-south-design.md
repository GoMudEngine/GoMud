## Overview

The first 10-room mini-stage of the North Road, branching west from
Ashwick Crossroads and curving north toward Stillwater. Farmland road
with a crossroads village, Betta's farmstead, and a bandit camp.

Two quests: "The Caravan Guard" (combat or morally gray diplomacy)
and "Betta's Silence" (exploration-triggered protection quest with
a roaming bloodline agent).

## Geography & Coordinates

The North Road heads west from Ashwick Crossroads (room 4014 at
-8, -13), then curves north through open farmland. The coordinate
corridor stays west of X = -9 to avoid Thornwall and Ironwind
overlap.

```

Dustwalk Road: X = -10 to -11, Y = -2 to 5 (no conflict)
Thornwall:     X = -1 to 7,    Y = -4 to 4 (no conflict)
```

## Room Descriptions

All rooms follow the quality standards in ZONE_EXPANSION.md:
- 80-character line width
- Three layers: atmosphere, physical detail, interaction hint
- Sensory variety (not all sight-led)
- At least 2 examinable nouns per room
- Season: late autumn. Farmland, hedgerows, open sky.

### Road Rooms

**Road Fork** — Where the Marches Spur meets the main highway.
A weathered signpost points north (Stillwater), south (Amber
Valley), and east (Ashwick). The road widens here — wagon ruts
deeper, the packed earth of heavier traffic. Examinable: signpost,
wagon ruts.

**Open Farmland** — Flat country stretching to the horizon.
Hedgerows divide fields of harvested stubble. The wind carries
the smell of turned earth and something faintly sweet — late
apples rotting under a tree somewhere out of sight. Examinable:
hedgerow, apple tree.

**Hedgerow Bend** — The road curves north around a dense
hedgerow. Farm gates line the eastern side. A boundary marker —
a squat stone pillar carved with the Chrysalis spiral — stands
at the bend, worn smooth in the center from years of hands laid
on it. Examinable: boundary marker (spiral description, "In this
territory, all who carry the gift are under the protection of
the Chrysalis"), farm gate.

**Rolling Fields** — The road rises and falls through gentle
terrain. From the crest you can see the road both ahead and
behind — a pale line through dark fields. A roadside shrine sits
at the high point, a simple stone niche with a faded offering
bowl. Examinable: shrine (posted notice about road safety —
Caravan Guard breadcrumb), the view (description of the road
stretching north).

**Crossroads Village Square** — A handful of buildings around
a muddy square. Smoke rises from the tavern chimney. A well
stands in the center, its bucket rope frayed. The buildings are
practical — stone foundations, timber upper floors, thatched
roofs patched with varying degrees of care. Examinable: well,
tavern sign (Haral's).

**North Road** — The road north of the village. Wider here,
the packed earth showing the weight of regular wagon traffic.
Boot prints and hoofprints overlap in the mud. A figure loiters
near a stand of scrub trees, watching the road. Examinable:
boot prints (mixed traveler and patrol prints), the figure
(the bandit lookout mob).

**Drainage Ditch** — An embankment runs alongside the road
here, heavy with the dead scrub of late autumn. A drainage
ditch cuts between the road and the fields. In the scrub, a
narrow gap where the branches have been recently disturbed —
someone crouched here. Examinable: disturbed scrub (traces of
someone hiding), drainage ditch, a path leading west toward a
farmstead.

### Tavern Interiors

**Common Room** — Low-ceilinged, warm, smelling of woodsmoke
and old ale. A fire crackles in a stone hearth. Rough tables
fill the room, most empty this time of day. A bar runs along
the back wall where Haral keeps his bottles in careful rows.
Examinable: hearth, bar, bottles.

**Kitchen** — Fat-smoke and old wood and the sharp green note
of herbs. A heavy work-table dominates the center, scarred from
years of chopping. A window looks out onto the back yard — the
sill is at chest height, a body-length drop to the flagstones
below. Examinable: window (the drop, recent scuff marks on the
sill), work-table, herb bundles.

**Back Yard** — A small flagstone yard behind the tavern,
hemmed in by a low wall. A few barrels lean against the wall.
The flagstones are uneven, one cracked where something heavy
landed. At the base of the wall below the kitchen window, a
faint scuff mark on the stone — someone landed here recently
and stumbled left. Examinable: cracked flagstone, scuff mark
(traces of a landing and stumble), barrels.

### Betta's Farmstead

**Betta's Path** — A dirt track leads west from the road through
a field of harvested turnip rows. A quarter-mile ahead, a low
farmstead hunches against the wind. Sheep dot the near field,
their wool thick for winter. The smell of lanolin and woodsmoke
carries on the breeze. Examinable: turnip field (impressive
purple turnips), sheep.

**Betta's Yard** — The farmhouse is stone-walled, low, built
to endure weather rather than impress. A front door and a side
path to the back. Chickens scratch in the packed dirt. A wooden
bench by the door has a half-finished whittling project on it —
a small bird with its wings tucked. Smoke curls from the chimney.
Examinable: whittling (Corvin's work — a bird with its head
tilted as if listening), farmhouse door, chickens.

**Betta's Barn** — Warm in the way of places where animals have
lived a long time — a deep settled warmth that has nothing to do
with fire. Two cows chew placidly in their stalls. A donkey
watches from the corner with ancient suspicion. Gap boards in the
walls let in thin bars of light. In the far corner, a makeshift
straw bedroll has been recently disturbed. Examinable: straw
bedroll (someone slept here recently — a few dark hairs, a
withered apple core), cows, donkey, gap boards.

### Bandit Camp

**Camp Approach** — A narrow track through dense scrub, barely
visible from the road. Boot prints in the mud — multiple sets,
coming and going. The scrub thickens overhead, blocking the sky.
The smell of a cold campfire ahead. Examinable: boot prints
(heavy, deliberate — not farmers), scrub (recently cut back to
widen the path).

**Bandit Camp** — A clearing hacked from the scrub. A firepit
with grey ash, still warm. Bedrolls in a rough circle. A
makeshift lean-to shelters supplies — rope, tools, a few
weapons. This is a working camp, not a hideout — these people
plan to stay. Examinable: firepit, supplies (rope, lockpicks,
a map of the road with waypoint markings), bedrolls.

## NPCs

### Friendly / Neutral

**Haral** (tavern-keeper)
- Location: Tavern common room
- Role: Merchant (food/drink), quest breadcrumb
- Personality: Heavyset, fifties, practical. Bought the tavern
  after his knees gave out from farming. Keeps opinions behind
  the bar. Knows everything on the road because everyone stops
  here.
- Dialogue topics: the village, road conditions, "the girl who
  worked the kitchen" (oblique — won't say much), the posted
  notice about bandits, food/drink for sale
- Non-combatant, charm_immune

**Old Dessa** (tavern regular, gossiper)
- Location: Tavern common room, by the hearth
- Role: Rumors/news system (modeled on Old Gobb)
- Groups: humanoid, gossiper
- Personality: Elderly former midwife. Sits by the hearth all
  day nursing one drink. Gossips freely about everything.
- Idle emotes: mutters to herself and shakes her head, adjusts
  her shawl closer against a draft only she feels, peers at new
  arrivals over the rim of her cup, taps the table impatiently
  as if waiting for someone
- Dialogue topics: travelers on the road, bloodline agents
  ("came through twice last month"), the bandits ("they've got a
  caster with them now"), "a woman north of the village who keeps
  to herself" (Betta breadcrumb)
- Non-combatant, charm_immune

**Tam** (tavern regular)
- Location: Tavern common room
- Role: Bandit camp breadcrumb
- Personality: Young farmhand, drinks too much, talks too loud.
- Idle emotes: laughs too loudly at nothing, waves his cup for a
  refill, leans back dangerously in his chair
- Dialogue topics: complains about the bandit toll on his
  employer's wagon, mentions the camp is "past the drainage ditch,
  follow the scrub." Can be wrong about details.
- Non-combatant

**Corvin** (Betta's son)
- Location: Road Fork room (with turnip cart)
- Role: Betta's farmstead breadcrumb, Betta's Silence quest entry
- Personality: ~10 years old, whittles wooden birds, shrewd about
  strangers. Protective of his mother without knowing he's doing
  it.
- Interaction: Player can help him with his stuck cart (simple
  action). This earns a small trust flag. He mentions "my mother
  makes a lot of soup" and gives directions to the farmstead.
- Non-combatant, charm_immune

**Betta** (farm wife)
- Location: Betta's Yard
- Role: Betta's Silence quest giver
- Personality: Late forties, compact, capable. Sheep farmer.
  Taciturn. Will not volunteer information. Sizes up everyone
  within three seconds of meeting them.
- Dialogue topics: the road, her sheep, the weather, her son.
  Does NOT mention the girl unless the player has examined the
  straw bedroll in the barn AND asks about it.
- Quest trigger: After player finds the bedroll trace and asks,
  Betta requests silence. "If anyone asks about someone staying
  in my barn, you didn't see anything."
- Non-combatant, charm_immune

**Caravan Master** (merchant)
- Location: Crossroads village square (or rolling fields road)
- Role: Caravan Guard quest giver
- Personality: Professional trader, pragmatic. Transporting goods
  to Stillwater. Concerned about the bandit threat.
- Dialogue topics: the road, his cargo, hiring protection, his
  brother/partner in Stillwater who runs a trading post.
- Non-combatant

**Ambient Farmers** (1-2 generic NPCs)
- Location: Wander between road rooms (Road Fork through Rolling
  Fields), MaxWander 3-4
- Role: Atmosphere
- Idle emotes: adjust a heavy sack, shield eyes against the wind,
  nod in passing
- Dialogue: crops, weather, "riders asking questions last week"
- Non-combatant

### Hostile Mobs

**Bandit Lookout** (rogue archetype)
- Location: North Road room (loiters near scrub trees)
- Role: Ambush trigger for Caravan Guard quest
- Behavior: Not initially hostile. Attacks if player is escorting
  caravan or if player attacks first. On combat start, uses
  `call_for_help` to pull camp bandits. Skullduggery skill,
  lighter/faster, carries a dagger.
- AI: ambusher preset. No scatter. Fights to the death or flees
  to camp if badly hurt.
- Stats: Step up from Thornwall highwaymen. Statpool ~55-60.

**Bandit Fighter** (fighting archetype)
- Location: Bandit Camp
- Role: Melee bruiser, responds to lookout's call
- Behavior: Charges in when called. Weapon-combat focused, sword
  and shield. No scatter — holds position.
- AI: aggressive_melee preset with scatter disabled.
- Stats: Statpool ~65-70. Tougher than anything in Thornwall.

**Bandit Caster** (casting archetype)
- Location: Bandit Camp
- Role: Debuffer, responds to lookout's call
- Behavior: Opens with nerve-disruption or mind-fog on player,
  then switches to damage spells. Stays at range.
- AI: defensive_caster preset.
- Spellbook: nerve-disruption, mind-fog, mind-spike, mend-wounds
  (self-heal).
- Stats: Statpool ~55-60. Fragile but dangerous.

**Soren** (bandit leader, fighting archetype)
- Location: Bandit Camp
- Role: Boss mob, diplomacy target
- Behavior: Fights if attacked. Can be talked to — has dialogue
  tree for the diplomacy path. Toughest mob in the zone.
- AI: aggressive_melee preset, no scatter, call_for_help when
  health drops below 30%.
- Dialogue (diplomacy path): Demands the sealed crate from the
  caravan. Claims the Caravan Master cheated his crew on a prior
  job. "I don't want blood. I want what's owed." Player can
  agree to get the crate (steal or convince the master), refuse
  (fight), or try to investigate (examine the crate — no clean
  answer either way).
- Stats: Statpool ~80. Named mob, better gear (quality sword,
  leather armor with physical_mitigation).

### Roaming Mob

**Bloodline Agent** (not hostile by default)
- Location: Spawns at Road Fork, roams road rooms. MaxWander 6-7.
- Role: Betta's Silence quest resolution
- Personality: Professional, polite, slightly menacing. Asking
  routine questions about road traffic.
- Behavior: Not hostile unless attacked. If attacked, fights back
  hard — high stats, not a farming target at this progression
  level.
- Dialogue: Asks the player about "a young woman, dark-haired,
  traveling north alone" and "anyone sheltering travelers off the
  road." Player response determines quest outcome.
- AI: No combat AI unless provoked. If provoked, fights with
  weapon-combat, high stats.
- Stats: Statpool ~100. Meant to discourage combat — this is not
  a mob to farm. Charm_immune.
- Groups: humanoid
- Non-combatant flag: false (can be attacked, but will destroy
  most players at this level)

## Quests

### Quest A: The Caravan Guard

**Quest ID:** TBD (next available)

**Entry points (3 breadcrumbs):**
1. Caravan Master asks directly (crossroads village)
2. Posted notice at the roadside shrine (Rolling Fields room)
3. Tam at the tavern mentions bandits and approximate camp
   location

**Flow:**

```
Player learns about bandits
         |
    ┌────┴────┐
    │         │
  Combat    Diplomacy
    │         │
  Escort    Find camp
  caravan    Talk to Soren
    │         │
  Lookout    Soren demands
  triggers   the crate
  ambush       │
    │      ┌───┴───┐
  Fight    Steal/   Refuse
  all 4    convince  (fight)
  bandits  master     │
    │      to give    Same as
    │      crate      combat
    │         │
  Master   Master
  grateful bitter
    │         │
  Token    Reduced
  for      gold,
  Stillwater no token
  discount
```

**Combat path:** Player escorts caravan north past the lookout.
Lookout uses call_for_help. Fighter, caster, and Soren converge.
4v1 fight (or with caravan guards if we add them). On victory,
Caravan Master gives gold + a letter of introduction to his
partner in Stillwater.

**Diplomacy path:** Player finds the camp before the caravan
moves. Soren explains the dispute. Player can:
- Get the crate: Either steal it or persuade the Caravan Master
  to hand it over (rhetoric check or repeated dialogue). Soren
  acknowledges the deal. Quest completes.
- Examine the crate: Identify or perception reveals valuable
  contents but doesn't prove who owns them. No clean answer.
- Refuse: Fight triggers.

**Resolution:**
- Quest state is per-player. Bandits respawn normally like any
  other mob zone — the camp is a permanent combat area. This
  ensures all players can discover and complete the quest
  independently. (Future instanced content may revisit this.)
- Combat: Full gold reward + Stillwater discount token.
- Diplomacy (gave crate): Reduced gold, no token. Soren gives
  grudging respect dialogue.
- Stillwater partner NPC (Zone 2.2) checks for the token and
  offers discounted prices if present.

**Quest re-grant prevention:** End token in questExcluded on all
granting nodes per SOP.

### Quest B: Betta's Silence

**Quest ID:** TBD (next available)

**Entry points (3 breadcrumbs):**
1. Corvin on the road — help with cart, he mentions the farmstead
2. Old Dessa at the tavern — mentions "a woman north of the
   village who keeps to herself"
3. The straw bedroll in Betta's barn — examinable, shows someone
   slept there recently

**Flow:**

```
Player explores barn
         |
    Finds bedroll trace
         |
    Asks Betta about it
         |
    Betta requests silence
    "You didn't see anything"
         |
    Bloodline agent roams road
         │
    ┌────┼────────┬──────────┐
    │    │        │          │
  Stay  Mis-    Tell       Kill
  quiet direct  agent      agent
    │    │        │          │
  Betta Betta   Agent     Betta
  trusts trusts  pays     angry
  player player  gold     "you've
    │    │        │       brought
  Carved Carved  Betta   attention"
  bird + bird +  goes      │
  lore   lore    cold    Refuses
                         to talk
```

**Trigger:** Player must examine the straw bedroll in the barn
(finds dark hairs, withered apple core). Then ask Betta about
"the barn" or "someone staying." Betta sizes them up and makes
the request.

**Resolution:** Four paths depending on what happens with the
bloodline agent:
- **Stay quiet / misdirect:** Betta gives the player a small
  carved wooden bird (Corvin's work) and a lore dialogue about
  the road north and the people who travel it. Trust earned.
- **Tell the agent:** Small gold reward from the agent. Betta's
  dialogue goes cold. Corvin won't speak to the player. No bird,
  no lore.
- **Kill the agent:** The agent drops combat loot (high-quality
  gear befitting a statpool 350 mob), but Betta is furious.
  "You think killing one of them helps? Now they'll send more.
  Now they'll pay attention to this stretch of road." She
  refuses to speak to the player further. Corvin watches from
  the doorway but says nothing. No bird, no lore. The quiet
  protection she built is broken by blunt force.

**Design intent:** No right answer is stated. The game shows
consequences, not judgments. The carved bird is a keepsake item
with flavor text, not a mechanical advantage. Killing the agent
is the "gamer" instinct and the game explicitly punishes it —
not mechanically, but narratively. Betta's whole philosophy is
that silence protects better than violence.

**Quest re-grant prevention:** End token in questExcluded per SOP.

## Mob Difficulty Progression

This zone targets players who are comfortably soloing Ironwind
cave mobs (statpool 140-200). The bandits individually are in
that range, but they fight as a coordinated group — the first
time players face multiple mobs working together tactically.
The group fight is the real difficulty spike, not individual
mob stats.

| Mob | Statpool | Archetype | Notable |
|-----|----------|-----------|---------|
| Bandit Lookout | 140 | fighting | Skullduggery, dagger, call_for_help |
| Bandit Fighter | 200 | fighting | Sword+shield, no scatter |
| Bandit Caster | 160 | casting | 4 spells, debuff opener |
| Soren (leader) | 250 | fighting | Named, better gear, diplomacy option |
| Bloodline Agent | 350 | fighting | NOT a farming target, discourages combat |

Bandits fight as a unit: no pack scatter, lookout pulls the
group via call_for_help. The caster debuffs while the fighter
and Soren close to melee. This is the first coordinated group
fight players encounter.

### Soren's Ironbound Buckler (new item)

A battered but well-maintained round shield, its iron rim scored
from years of hard use. Soren clearly valued this above anything
else in the camp.

- **Slot:** Offhand (shield)
- **Type:** shield
- **Subtype:** buckler
- **physical_mitigation:** 8
- **magical_mitigation:** 4
- **knockdown_chance:** 10 (small bonus to bash/trip knockdown)
- **block_bonus:** 5
- **Value:** ~80 gold
- Fills the gap between "no shield" and the Windstone Aegis.
  A solid mid-tier option that most players would use until
  they get something better from later content.

### Other Loot Items

- **Bandit's Leather Vest:** Light armor, physical_mitigation 5,
  wearable. Decent but not best-in-slot for anything.
- **Bandit's Longsword:** weapon, damage_multiplier 1.1, speed
  0.9. Slightly better than shop swords.
- **Lockpick Set:** Utility item (future skullduggery use or
  sell value).

### Quest Reward Items

- **Carved Wooden Bird** (Betta's Silence): Keepsake item,
  flavor text only. "A small whittled bird with its wings tucked
  and head tilted, as if listening." No mechanical benefit.
- **Letter of Introduction** (Caravan Guard, combat path):
  Consumed when presented to the Stillwater trading post partner.
  Unlocks discount pricing.
