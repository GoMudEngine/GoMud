 ┌─ .:Description ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   Toke is the town's voice, and he comes by the work honestly, for he was
   born with twice the means to do it. He stands the square from first
   light to last, reading the notice-post, gathering the day's word off
   every traveler and gossip that crosses the slabs, and giving it all back
   louder than he got it. Below his proper mouth sits a second, set low at
   the throat, and it is the loud one -- it carries the call across the
   square while the upper mouth shapes the smaller talk, so that he can
   hail a newcomer and finish a sentence to someone else in the same
   breath. He means no harm and keeps no secrets worth keeping; ask him
   anything and he has likely already told three other people. He has news
   for you before you have thought to want it, and a grin for the asking. 
   Crier Toke is in perfect health.

   Tally runs the Drowned Lantern the way a good current runs a river --
   you never see the work, only that everything arrives where it ought to.

   someone who has run this room so long it runs to her hand.

   who jump.

 ┌─ .:Description ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   Croup keeps the Strongbox House and the great balanced book that runs
   it, and he does both with a dry, exact care that strangers mistake for

===== ADDENDUM 2026-06-12: 5207 re-capture after shop pricing fix =====
What changed & why: fresh shops used to seed Current = RestockQty, leaving the
scarcity ratio at 1.0 (the curve's ~2.36x price), so Onna opened at inflated
prices (e.g. Sling listed 95g on base 40) and paid ~1.18x value for player
sells (fresh-shop arbitrage). RegisterShop now seeds Current at the abundance
level (min(MaxStock, ceil(RestockQty x AbundanceThreshold))) — the long-run
steady state of an established shop — so a brand-new vendor opens fully stocked
at floor (0.25x) prices. Onna's YAML also had decorative quantity/quantitymax/
price fields that the living-economy path ignores; those were stripped.
Below: list now shows ~15 units at floor prices, and a buy charges exactly the
listed price (note Trail Rations shows 14 — one was bought in a prior run of
