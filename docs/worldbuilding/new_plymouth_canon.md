# New Plymouth — Canon Reference (from *What the Moons Keep*)

> Source bible for the New Plymouth city build. Extracted from
> `what_the_moons_keep.md`. The MUD is set **after** the novel: the
> protagonists (Maren, Davan, Vane, Aldric) and the core cooperage group
> have left east; the city bears their traces. Use this for location,
> faction, mutation, voice, and sensory fidelity. Master plan:
> `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-20-new-plymouth-design.md`.

## 1. City geography (canon)

**Orientation:** The river cuts from the **northeast** into a harbor that
opens **west to the sea**. The river road runs along the bank.

- **Docks District** — western waterfront, quarter-mile of dock; seven
  neighborhoods visible from the quay; shadow economy in plain sight.
- **Temple District** — **EAST**, on the southern ridge above the river;
  older, spacious, elevated. Switchback ridge road descends to the lower city.
- **Crafting Quarter** — between docks and center; the **Inkwalk** (curving
  lane of mapmakers/scribes, a chandler, a bookseller), smithies, glassblowers.
  The cooperage lane is "two streets from the river."
- **Canal District / Old Quarter** — pre-colonial stonework beneath/adjacent
  to Crafting & docks; **Lintel Street** runs parallel to the canal, ducks
  under a stone footbridge, narrows. **This is the MUD's "Sewers" zone.**
- **Merchant Quarter** — central commercial; "four districts from the rooming
  house near the old seminary"; Horst's territory.
- **Noble Quarter** — far end from the docks; bloodline private addresses
  (held through three nominal owners); implied hollow detention.
- **The Palace** — **northern rise**; enormous, newer than the temple, "far
  more concerned with being seen to be where it was"; visible from the river
  on arrival; 300 years of unbroken bloodline occupation. The palace steps
  bear a burn-black scar from the Stilling that no changed person can look at.
- **Carter's Rise** — a rise with a public well and flower-market at the top;
  north stair down to the **tannery streets** (near Crafting/Common).
- **The old seminary** + **Tova's rooming house** — a quiet street, window
  onto an alley, view of the seminary wall, river smell at all hours.

**Two ways into the city:**
- **East Gate** — main road, **watched**; bloodline domestic-office agents
  monitor it; midday shift-change has a search brief. → MUD attach: Outskirts
  East Gate (room 5471).
- **The Ford** — **two miles west of the east gate**, no toll, no permanent
  post; comes out on the river road; the unwatched way in (Vane's standard).
  → MUD attach: Outskirts River Road (room 5480) → Docks.

**Skyline:** ruled by two landmarks — the **temple** (east, ancient) and the
**palace** (northern rise, built to be seen from the river approach).

**Named streets:** Cutter's Lane (docks alley), Lintel Street (canal),
the Inkwalk (Crafting), Sellcross Road (4 streets N of the cooperage),
Marlin's Walk (3 streets S of the cooperage), the river road, the harbor road.

**Scale:** "a hundred thousand lives." Enormous, unknowable to newcomers.

## 2. Named characters

> Tier key for the build: **STAYS** = placeable resident NPC post-novel;
> **LEFT** = departed east, referenced as lore/traces only; **ANTAGONIST** =
> active threat still present.

### Edvar Marrick — mapmaker (LEFT; shop is a trace)
*E. Marrick, Cartographer and Chartmaker.* Crafting Quarter, on the Inkwalk.
~60, lean, scholar's hands, ink-stained cuticles, merchant's eyes. Precise
gatekeeper — *"You're someone Pip vouched for on a Thursday. That's a start,
not an arrival."* Spent 20 years on the fourth-moon question; kept the "face"
(ancient display device) in an iron-banded trunk. Left east with the group.
**Post-novel:** shop shuttered or taken over; the Inkwalk remembers him.

### Asha — glassblower (LEFT)
Crafting Quarter. ~30 or younger; heat-worker's forearms, scarred at the
wrists. Direct, drops single words "like a stone, on target." Cooperage member.

### Renner — former temple archivist, cooperage leader (LEFT)
~50, "aggressive precision," gray-threaded dark hair, straight spine. Left the
temple archive "after too long in the eastern catalog asking the wrong
questions about the Restricted Collection." Runs the Thursday meetings.

### Corvel — tanner, cooperage member (LEFT)
Square-built, chemical-stained knuckles, deliberate speech. Carried the gray
container for 8 years — found it "in the marshland south of the ridge,
half-buried in clay."

### Tela — young cartographer (LEFT)
Apprentice; ink-stained writing hand; intent silence. Rode decoy through the
east gate with an Inkwalk boy on departure.

### Pip — dock finder (LEFT)
Docks, born there. 23, "built like a question mark." Mutation: **accurate
intuition about where a lost/hidden thing is likely to be** (not prediction).
The cooperage's social connector; introduced Davan to Edvar.

### Jesset — dock foreman (STAYS)
Docks. Large, weathered. Mutation: **an additional joint in each finger** —
extraordinary grip, memorable handshake. Hires day-labor.

### Cade — Bloom-trade operator (STAYS / disrupted)
Outer edge of the Crafting Quarter. Small, precise, long gray hair,
**"hands very clean."** Front: fabric-remnants shop with a back room reached by
a changing knock. Reported to the dock constabulary by Vane — **post-novel his
operation is disrupted; a successor may be filling the vacuum.**

### Deren — older Bloom supplier (STAYS / exposed)
Canal district. Early 50s, quick still eyes, merchant-not-fighter. Ran the
**hollow-captive production room at 215 Lintel Street**; key on a cord inside
his shirt. Surrendered the key to Vane. **Post-novel: exposed, the basement a
discoverable crime scene.**

### Tova — landlady (STAYS)
Runs the rooming house near the old seminary. Discreet, experienced with people
who need a room and silence at once. **Holds the stories of "the group that left."**

### Horst — bloodline domestic-office handler (ANTAGONIST, STAYS)
Merchant Quarter rented house, two agents. Seal: pressed coin, not wax.
Systematic — *"thoroughness... showed as symmetry when the net was wide, and
overlap when the net was closing."* Agents wear too-plain gray-brown coats.
**Post-novel: still in NP, still hunting; agents went east.** A live threat the
player can run afoul of.

### Yelin — lay brother / warden, Keeper's House (STAYS)
Temple District, secondary chapel of Saint Imret the Patient. 19+ years there.

### Father Thane — desk intake, Keeper's House (STAYS)
Temple District administrative desk.

### "Junie" — freed Bloom captive (LEFT, south)
Young, dark hair, small white scar at the mouth-corner, hollow. Freed by Vane;
went south along the coast road with a coin sewn into a shoe.

### Protagonists (all LEFT east — traces only)
- **Vane** — tracker, six-year docks resident, **bark-skin mutation** spreading
  up the right arm, olfactory super-sense, Bloom-dependent. Two rooms over the
  street; the rooming house refuge.
- **Maren** — 17, hollow (unchanged), carries her father's pulsing disc.
- **Davan** — ~20, carver, **skin-light mutation** (bioluminescence shifting
  with emotion). Docks labor → cooperage basement.
- **Aldric Voss** — Keeper from the Confluence, 30-year career, traveling plain.

## 3. Key locations

- **The Cooperage** (Crafting, river road, two streets from the river): plain
  working building; front shop (apprentice with steam-reddened hands sells a
  hoop-set as the cover purchase, nods you through a door behind the stave
  rack); **the yard** (slatted fence, gate to a service alley running north to
  the river road, climbable midpoint wall, stacked barrel-hoops, packed earth);
  **the basement** (stone stairs, low-ceilinged, mineral cold, two lamps, edges
  in shadow, ~7–9 chairs, building-sounds through the stone). The cooperage
  group's meeting place. **Post-novel: empty, discoverable, lore-dense.**
- **Edvar's shop** (Inkwalk): narrow deep front room, scroll-racks, drafting
  table under a north window, pigment jars by hue, charts in vertical cubbies
  "like a library built sideways"; back quarters behind a curtain (cot, iron-
  banded trunk that held the "face"). Posted hours "dawn to second bell."
- **Cade's supply house** (outer Crafting): fabric-remnants front; back room via
  a storage-cupboard door + changing knock.
- **215 Lintel Street** (canal/Old Quarter): iron-banded door, pattern knock,
  heavy doorman; seven steps down to a stone corridor (two oil lamps, low
  ceiling); the production room (8×6 paces, stone, a drain in the floor, a
  pallet, collection apparatus — glass, copper tubing, wax-sealed clay pots);
  Deren's ledger room above (creaking 4th & 7th steps). **The Bloom Trail
  payoff.**
- **The rooming house near the old seminary** (Tova's): one bed, one table, one
  lamp; alley window; river smell; an established alley signal system.
- **Vane's rooms** (Docks): two rooms over the street; locked chests; lamp low.
- **Aldric's public house** (Docks, two blocks up from the waterfront): yard for
  horses, scarred-lip landlord, "quick uninterested kindness," no questions.
- **Davan's flophouse** (Docks): "better than a stable, not by much."
- **Carter's Rise square**: public well, flower-market; north stair to the
  tannery streets; site of a public hollow-accusation scene.
- **The market** (near the docks/fishmonger quarter): flower stall, dried-fish
  stalls; where Horst's agents work a spread pattern.
- **Horst's rented house** (Merchant Quarter): the search's command point.
- **The Noble Quarter address**: a private door (not a guardhouse); bloodline
  property three owners deep; where hollow people were delivered.
- **The Keeper's House** (Temple, chapel of Saint Imret the Patient): narrow
  stone hall, gated yard, warden Yelin, desk Father Thane.
- **The Grand Temple**: enormous, very old; rules the eastern skyline.
- **The NP Archive / Restricted Collection** (Temple): bloodline Founding-era
  catalog at "shelf 7-eastern"; access needs bloodline approval; a margin note
  in a non-archivist hand reads *"for unchanged hands."*
- **The Palace** (northern rise): visible from the river; the burn-black
  Stilling scar on the steps.

## 4. Factions & groups

- **The Cooperage Circle** (LEFT, joinable-via-lore post-novel): Renner, Edvar,
  Asha, Corvel, Pip, Tela (+ Davan, Maren, Aldric). Believe the inner-orbit
  symbol predates Chrysalis theology and is astronomical; the fourth moon came
  down; the gray-material artifacts are its debris; "changed hands only" is a
  political lie — the real qualifier may be **unchanged** hands. Meet Thursdays
  after second bell in the cooperage basement (hoop-set cover purchase → nod →
  door behind the stave rack). **Post-novel: the room remains; the secret is
  recoverable by an attentive player.**
- **The Bloom Trade**: hollow people captured and put into induced sleep;
  their secretion (copper-floral at source) collected, refined into thin
  iridescent near-odorless wafers; sold through suppliers (Cade in the docks/
  Crafting edge; Deren the deeper canal-district operation). Users get a 4-hour
  "communion" high; cost is the spreading bark-skin mutation + brutal
  withdrawal. **Bloodline domestic office runs the captive-production side.**
  **Post-novel: disrupted by Vane's constabulary report; a vacuum to be
  refilled = a live questline.**
- **Bloodline Domestic Office** (ANTAGONIST): field arm of governance, distinct
  from the church; runs contractors via disc-glyph; gate checkpoints; reach into
  the Keeper's House; Noble Quarter property; controls the Restricted
  Collection. **Horst is its NP presence.**
- **Church / Temple hierarchy**: Keeper's House (Yelin, Thane); the Archive
  holding the Restricted Collection; formally separate from the bloodline but
  intertwined at the Restricted-Collection level.

## 5. Plottable scenes (quest seeds)

- **Arrival by barge / by road** — the city swallows newcomers; smell before
  sight.
- **Dock labor + the crate catch** — Jesset hires; a falling crate; a friendship
  begins. → docks fetch/carry chain.
- **Cade's back room** — overhear "if she survives the first month." → Bloom
  Trail breadcrumb.

  something going around something." → information-broker beat.
- **Multi-visit access to Edvar's** — "come back when you've been here longer."
  → cooperage induction structure (now a lore reconstruction post-novel).
- **Hollow accusation at Carter's Rise** — a working deacon tests a porter; the
  crowd assembles and disperses in minutes. → public hazard for hollow PCs.
- **Market surveillance** — Horst's agents in a spread pattern. → evasion beat.
- **The cooperage basement meeting** — the container passes the table. →
  faction-induction / lore-dump.
- **Vane reports Cade to the constabulary** — blow up an operation to buy time.
  → high-stakes favor with faction consequences.
- **Edvar reveals the face** — the device lights; four orbital shapes. →
  central lore reveal.
- **Maren's disc pulses** — the test case. → hollow-PC cooperage climax.
- **Freeing the Bloom captive** — Deren's basement; coerce or fight; walk the
  captive out arm-in-arm. → rescue dungeon + the Bloom network chain.
- **Aldric & the pre-filed cover story** — Crane filed an entry in his name
  ahead of him. → temple spy-thriller beat.
- **The departure** — the ford, not the gate; decoy riders. → escort/distraction.

## 6. Sensory / atmosphere (for room-description fidelity)

- **Approaching by river:** *"the accumulated chemical fact of a hundred
  thousand lives lived close together for a very long time. It reached him a
  mile out and didn't let up."* Slate roofs, smoke haze, a gridwork older at
  the center and newer toward the walls.
- **Docks:** *"Brine and tar and the sweetness of rotting wood that had soaked
  in seawater for decades, and under everything a warm animal smell from the
  livestock pens two streets over."* Gulls arguing; stevedores; fish "cut and
  gutted and salted and smoked and newly-caught all braided together."
- **Crafting Quarter:** *"the compound of sawdust and metal and oil...
  matter-of-fact and indifferent."*
- **Edvar's shop:** *"linseed and old paper... the settled, sweet undertone of
  decades of it soaked into wood and plaster."*
- **Cooperage basement:** oak shavings and vinegar from above mixing with *"the
  mineral cold of a space that had been below ground a long time"*; mallets on
  hoops through the floorboards.
- **Canal / Lintel Street:** pre-colonial stone "the city had paved over and
  built on top of and grudgingly preserved." Dusk: grey transaction corridor.
  Morning: cart traffic, children chasing a dog, barge captains arguing rope
  prices.
- **Bloom basement:** *"copper-flower... at volume. At source. The way a
  tannery smells nothing like a leather glove."*
- **Temple District:** *"the deliberate spaciousness of institutions that had
  claimed their ground early and never been asked to yield it"* — bodies at
  rest or in ceremony, vs. the docks' bodies at work.
- **Night, from the rooming house:** the river smell at all hours; *"the moons
  rose one by one over the eastern roofline."*
- **Carter's Rise:** the crowd's certainty evaporating *"like water from a
  cracked basin"* the moment the deacon's test confirms the change.
